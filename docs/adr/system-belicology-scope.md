# ADR — Belicology Beyond Warfare

**Status:** Adopted by the project creator, 2026-10-10
**Scope:** Belicología / Belicology; specialization scope and its derived technique identity
**Related:** `data/system/specializations-catalog.yaml`, `docs/system/specializations.md`, `data/system/specialization-technique-domains.yaml`, `docs/system/specialization-technique-domains.md`, `docs/adr/system-abilities-and-specializations.md`

## Decision

Belicology studies the technical, tactical, strategic, psychological, and organizational principles of combat. It includes how cultures develop martial traditions and defensive methods in response to threats. Warfare between intelligent species is one context; defense against beasts and protection of communities also belong to the domain. Neither a war nor an army is required.

This replaces the catalog's warfare-only scope. It does not supersede the distinction between raw specialization use and trained Techniques in the abilities-and-specializations ADR.

## Boundaries and invariants

- Analysis requires relevant information and knowledge. Psychology does not grant access to hidden intentions or perfect predictions.
- Understanding a method does not grant weapon competency, a Technique, or the ability to execute it.
- Live command remains Leadership; general subsistence remains Survival. Immediate improvisation is not equivalent to structured combat analysis.
- Intellect, the active action classification, threshold resolution, existing penalties, ranks, progression, and technique prerequisites remain unchanged.
- Existing military examples remain valid; they no longer define the whole domain.

## Example

A character examines why a community uses long weapons, a narrow passage, and relief shifts to contain a beast. Belicology can explain the defensive method and its dependencies. It does not automatically teach the character to use those weapons or command the defenders.
