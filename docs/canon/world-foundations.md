# World Foundations

---

## The survival premise

The playable species are not dominant. They are among the weakest creatures in
the world. Their only advantage over everything else is intelligence — the same
advantage real humans have in a world full of things that can kill them.

The critical difference: human intelligence was sufficient to eventually dominate
the real world. Here, it is not. The tools they build, the walls they raise, the
protocols they develop — all of it is not enough to make the world safe. It is
enough to survive another season.

**Settlements are refuges.** They are concentrations of individuals where the
collective — more eyes, more hands, more warning — creates a margin of survival
that doesn't exist alone. They don't project power outward. They hold the line
inward.

**The space between settlements is hostile by default.** Not dangerous if you are
careless — hostile as a baseline condition. Most of what leaves a settlement
doesn't return. The ones who know the routes between settlements know them because
they survived learning them, not because anyone taught them safely.

**The gap between biological life expectancy and actual life expectancy is
enormous.** A species may be capable of living two hundred years. Most of its
population will not see one hundred. The two hundred-year-old individual is a
rarity that other settlements know by name. The world did not allow that to be
normal.

**Mythology, theology, and ritual exist as survival technology.** Archives record
what killed people and how. Ritual creates collective coherence under chronic
terror. Oral tradition preserves what the archive didn't reach in time. Culture
is not a luxury that emerges after survival is guaranteed — it is one of the
mechanisms by which survival is attempted.

## The Tauma as fundamental physics

The Tauma is not something that appeared after a catastrophe. It is a property
of the universe, as fundamental as gravity. It has always existed. It exists in
the Limbo — a parallel dimension where its concentration is indefinitely greater
than in the physical world — and the difference in concentration creates a
continuous gradient: the Tauma flows from the Limbo toward the physical world
passively and without ceasing.

The world has always had Primordials. It has always had Anomalías. It has always
been shaped by this energy that most of its inhabitants cannot perceive directly
and none of the current species can manipulate.

No mortal creature — not the playable species, not any animal — can interact with
the Tauma directly. They cannot detect it, accumulate it, or act on it. The only
mechanisms through which a mortal can have any relationship with the Tauma are
vestigos (residual Tauma-imprints in objects) and vínculos (active connections to
Limbo entities through objects). In both cases, it is not the mortal acting on
the Tauma — it is a Limbo entity using the mortal's nervous system as the channel
through which it crosses into the physical world.

What species have observed, empirically and over generations, is the physical
effects that Tauma concentration produces in matter, in environments, and in
biological tissue. They work with those effects the same way someone uses a tool
without understanding its internal mechanism: they know what it does, not what it
is.

## The human era

Millions of years before the current world, a species called humans existed on
this planet. They were, biologically, similar to the playable species in one
crucial way: intelligence was their primary edge. Unlike the current playable
species, however, humans eventually solved the dominance problem. They became the
apex species of their world through accumulated technology.

At some point in their history, humans discovered how to control the Tauma
directly. No one living knows what that technology was, how it worked, or what
it required. Its ruins are scattered across the world, built from materials that
have survived millions of years precisely because they were constructed using
Tauma-manipulation at a structural level. Those materials are extraordinarily
durable. They are also extraordinarily dangerous.

With Tauma control, human technology advanced at a rate that has no equivalent in
anything the current world produces. They eventually understood that the Tauma was
not a planetary property but a universal one — that the Limbo exists alongside the
entire physical universe, not just alongside this world. Multiple human
civilizations entered a race to access the Tauma beyond the planet's gradient,
pulling it from the wider universe to fuel increasingly large-scale projects.

This was done irresponsibly, in competition, and without understanding what
pulling Tauma from outside the planetary system would do to the equilibrium the
world had maintained for geological time.

The result: the planet's rotation reversed. When a planetary body reverses its
rotation, everything on its surface is ejected at the velocity the surface had
been traveling — in the order of thousands of kilometers per hour. All complex
life on the surface died instantaneously. The cataclysm was not a war or a
plague. It was a physics event caused by misuse of a force the humans could
control but did not fully understand.

The planet survived. The atmosphere destabilized and eventually restabilized
differently. The oceans reorganized. The geography changed. The human ruins — the
inorganic, Tauma-infused structures built to last — remained, scattered across
the reshaped world, now deeply contaminated with residual Tauma from whatever
the humans had been doing inside them.

## The re-emergence of life

Not everything died. The rotation reversal destroyed surface life. Organisms in
deep ocean trenches, deep underground, and in conditions extreme enough to survive
the ejection event persisted. These were the ancestors of all current life.

The Tauma, meanwhile, continued its fundamental behavior: flowing from the Limbo
into the physical world. With no living channels available — no nervous systems
for it to cross through — the Primordials could not fulfill their basic nature.
The gradient continued. The Tauma accumulated in a dead world.

The Abismales are Primordials that reached ecosystemic scale before the
cataclysm. They are not biological and did not die. They continued to be their
zones — the geology, chemistry, and physical organization of their territories
continuing to express their nature even without life present. Over geological
time, with Tauma saturating dead matter and Abismal zones organizing local
conditions according to their nature, the surviving deep-life began to
proliferate in those conditions.

Life did not re-emerge because the universe values life. It re-emerged because
the physics of the Tauma system tends toward the state that allows it to
express through the physical world — and that state requires living channels.
The organisms that survived were shaped by evolution in a world saturated with
Tauma residue from the human era plus the natural Tauma gradient, producing the
current ecology over millions of years.

**The playable species have no memory of this.** Their oldest mythologies begin
in the current world. Any belief about origins is constructed from what they
can observe and what they find in ruins they cannot understand. The most
sophisticated researchers among them have fragments of evidence — anomalous
Tauma patterns, ruins that suggest something incomprehensible happened —
but no living being has an accurate picture of the deep history of the world.

## The ecology: mortal, Anomalía, playable

Three categories of creature exist in the current world. They share the same
ultimate evolutionary origin — the survivors of the cataclysm — but diverged
under different evolutionary pressures.

### Mortal animals

Non-intelligent animals whose evolutionary development occurred in areas of
moderate or low Tauma concentration. The Tauma exists in their environment but
was not the dominant evolutionary pressure. They function according to normal
biological logic: predator and prey, territorial behavior, seasonal patterns.
They are not primarily threats to the playable species — they are part of the
world's ecosystem, prey animals, sources of materials, companions, and
occasionally dangerous in the way any large predator is dangerous.

### Anomalías

Animals biologically identical in base structure to their mortal equivalents,
whose evolutionary lineage spent sufficient generations in high-Tauma
concentration zones for the Tauma to become structurally integrated into their
biology. A mortal fox and an Anomaly fox share the same organs, the same
predator instincts, the same reproductive cycle. The Anomaly fox additionally
has the Tauma flowing through its body because its ancestors evolved in a zone
where that was the dominant environmental pressure.

The danger Anomalías represent is not merely physical capability. It is:

- **Tauma-based effects**: Contact with an Anomaly exposes a creature to the
  Tauma it carries, which can cause afflictions in beings with complex nervous
  systems.
- **Elemental orientation**: An Anomaly reflects the elemental nature of the
  zone where its lineage evolved. A creature from a fire-Tauma zone behaves
  and acts in ways organized around that nature, not just predator logic.
- **Primordial symbiosis**: In Primordial territory, Anomalías of high
  integration are functional expressions of the Primordial's nature. They
  don't follow instinct — they follow the gradient of the entity that
  organized their zone, without any of them knowing that is what they are
  doing.

Anomaly integration exists on a spectrum. Low-integration Anomalías are barely
distinguishable from their mortal equivalents and only marginally more
dangerous. High-integration Anomalías may be physically unrecognizable as their
base species and capable of Tauma effects significant enough to affect an entire
encounter.

### Playable species

Intelligent species whose evolutionary pressure produced intelligence rather than
Tauma integration. They are biologically mortal — no structural Tauma in their
biology at birth — and their advantage over everything else is cognition,
sociality, and the accumulated knowledge that cognition and sociality make
possible.

The evolutionary lines that produced the playable species are fully occupied by
those species. There are no mortal rhinoceroses because that lineage became the
Ceratox. There are no mortal elephants because that lineage became the Loxod.
Other animal lineages exist in both mortal and Anomaly variants; the lineages
that went to intelligence did not branch back.

## Bio-organic technology

The technology of the current world is bio-organic: the combination of living
organisms with inert materials to harness biological functions as tools,
weapons, infrastructure, and medicine.

This is not magic. It is empirical knowledge of what organisms do and how their
behavior can be directed, cultivated, or amplified. A filtration system is an
organism that filters, cultivated in a vessel. Armor with protective properties
uses organisms that harden under mechanical stress integrated into a structural
matrix. Medicine is often a cultivated microbial community applied to tissue
that the body can work with. Lighting comes from bioluminescent organisms
maintained in sealed containers.

**The central limitation:** organisms have needs. They respond to their
environment. They die. A piece of bio-organic equipment is not durable in the
way inert equipment is — it requires maintenance, the right temperature,
nutrients, specific conditions. Equipment that functions in a warm humid
settlement may fail in a cold dry mountain pass because the organism component
cannot survive the transition. This creates constant maintenance pressure that
competes with survival pressure.

**Why this technology and not another:** The current species cannot replicate
human technology. They do not understand it. What they can do is observe how
living things behave and build on that observation. The bio-organic approach
is what intelligence applied to available materials produced over generations of
necessity.

**Scale:** Bio-organic technology ranges from simple (a fungus applied to
wounds that reduces infection) to complex (multi-organism assemblies engineered
for specific combat or industrial functions). The most sophisticated current
technology is still incomparably primitive relative to what the human ruins
suggest was once possible.

## Human ruins

Human ruins are not ancient cities that have fallen to ruin in the usual sense.
They are the remnants of structures built using direct Tauma-manipulation, made
from materials that survived a planetary cataclysm because they were constructed
under principles no living being understands.

They cannot be used. Nothing inside a human ruin is extractable, repurposable,
or safe to interact with. The construction materials themselves — built with
Tauma-manipulation no living being understands — cannot be worked, harvested,
or incorporated into any bio-organic process. Attempts to do so have uniformly
failed, often fatally. The technology that produced them is not recoverable
because it is not legible: no current species has any framework for understanding
what those materials are doing or why they behave as they do.

**As death trap:** Human ruins contain extreme vestige density — objects whose
Tauma imprint from human-era operations has not yet fully dissipated — plus
embedded vínculos whose linked entities may still be active. The danger is not
the ambient Tauma concentration, which is passive and pre-conscious. The danger
is the cumulative pressure of hundreds of active vestiges in close proximity,
and the Limbo entities behind vínculos that continue to use whatever nervous
systems enter the space. Entering a ruin without understanding what is inside —
which objects are still active, what sense each requires, what entity is on the
other side of each vínculo — is one of the most reliable ways to acquire severe
afflictions. The objects inside do not announce what they do. They simply do it
when the conditions they were built to respond to are met, and those conditions
are not legible from the outside.

**As cultural raw material:** Because the playable species have no memory of
the human era, human ruins are interpreted rather than understood. A settlement
that builds near a ruin, that uses its walls as a foundation, that incorporates
its artifacts into ritual — is not doing so with any knowledge of what those
things were. The mythology, the theology, the cultural identity that emerges
from this is constructed meaning applied to incomprehensible objects. This is
not false — it is what culture has always been. But it means that some of the
most foundational beliefs of some species rest on a misreading of something
that was never intended to communicate anything to them.

## Umbral materials

Umbral materials are naturally occurring matter found in zones of very high
extranatural concentration — places where the Tauma gradient is so intense that
it has structurally altered the physical properties of the matter in the zone
over geological time. These are not human-made materials. They are what
natural matter becomes when it has spent long enough in conditions no living
species deliberately created.

Zones of this kind exist independently of human ruins, though ruins tend to
be among the highest-concentration environments because of what was happening
in them during the human era. Primordial territories can also produce these
conditions. Other high-Tauma anomaly zones exist without any human-era origin.

Umbral materials have fixed, documented extranatural properties — they behave
predictably once identified, but cannot be controlled or directed by a user.
Their properties are the result of the Tauma that saturated them, not of
anything the user is doing. Working with umbral materials is like working with
an extremely dangerous substance that happens to have useful fixed behaviors:
the danger is intrinsic to the zone from which it came, the yield is limited,
and the extraction process has a high mortality rate.

## Design implications

**For encounter design:**
- The environment is a threat, not just the creatures in it. High ambient Tauma
  concentration does not directly cause afflictions — the critical factor is
  whether that Tauma is organized. Primordial territory produces afflictions
  through sustained exposure because the flow there is not pre-conscious but
  directed by the entity. Ruins produce afflictions through extreme vestige
  density, not through ambient Tauma concentration. Abismal territory
  functions as the body of the entity — the terrain, weather, and flora serve
  its nature. A ruin is not a set piece; it is an active Tauma environment.
- Anomalía difficulty scales naturally with integration level. Low-integration
  Anomalías are manageable threats. High-integration Anomalías in Primordial
  territory are operating as part of a system that the players cannot fully
  read.
- The players' species are physically weak relative to the threats they face.
  Combat solutions are dangerous. Avoidance, preparation, and knowledge are
  as important as technique.

**For world and species design:**
- No species has accurate knowledge of deep history. Their understanding of
  the world is empirical and mythological, not comprehensive.
- Culture and theology are survival technology. When designing a species'
  beliefs, the question is: what survival problem did this belief system
  solve well enough to persist?
- Bio-organic technology has hard environmental limits. Any technology that
  appears in the setting should have a maintenance or environmental dependency
  that creates pressure.
- Human ruins are not decorative. They are active Tauma environments that
  change what is safe in their vicinity. A ruin near a settlement changes the
  settlement's ecology, its theology, its threat profile.
