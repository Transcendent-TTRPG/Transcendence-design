# Loxod

## 1. Identity

The Loxod cannot stop receiving.

Their olfactory system connects directly to memory and emotion without cortical mediation — the same architectural shortcut that exists in smaller form in most mammals, but in the Loxod developed to an extreme that changes the terms of what it means to inhabit a body. They absorb the chemical record of intense events: stress hormones, trauma compounds, the specific molecular signature of death. Their nervous system does not classify these inputs and file them. It reconstructs them as experience. A Loxod who enters a space where something significant happened does not observe that something significant happened — they receive the event, involuntarily, as though it were happening now.

They also emit continuously. Their laryngeal anatomy produces infrasound below the threshold of most species' perception, transmitting through air and ground simultaneously. In the world as they know it, this is how Loxod have always communicated at distance — a seismic channel that carries across terrain, through walls, between individuals who cannot see or hear each other. What they did not design is that this same channel overlaps with the vibration range that vestige patterns use when they express through the world's physical media. The universe was always saturated with Tauma; objects accumulate consciousness imprints over centuries; those imprints anchor into crystallized patterns that express through vibration, pressure, sound, and light. Loxod biology evolved within this world and converged, through ordinary selection, on the same frequencies those patterns use. The Loxod do not perceive Tauma directly through this channel — what they receive is their own signal returning, modified by the patterns it passed through. The consequence is that entities whose expression operates in those frequencies can hear them.

Neither of these facts is supernatural. Both are biology. What makes them significant is that the Loxod are intelligent enough to understand exactly what they are.

Their civilization — the archives, the encrypted communication systems, the hierarchies defined by what a person can safely carry — is not academic culture that developed alongside this biology. It is the institutional response to it. They became scholars because the alternative to organizing what you absorb involuntarily is being destroyed by it. The architecture of their cities is resonance management infrastructure before it is anything else. The elaborate encoding systems for their knowledge are not vanity or secrecy — they are a protocol developed over centuries for how to hold information that resonates without letting the resonance leak uncontrolled into shared space.

The civilizational question the Loxod carry without resolving: **they are building toward something, but they cannot be certain that what they are building is wisdom, or simply the most elaborate structure they have found for containing what they cannot stop accumulating.**

## 2. Body

The Loxod are large bipedal humanoids descended from elephant stock. The basic body plan is humanoid — bilateral symmetry, two arms, two legs, upright posture — but the proportions and anatomy are unmistakably elephant-derived. They stand between 2.5 and 3 meters; they weigh between 400 and 500 kilograms. The skin is thick and deeply pigmented, ranging from gray-charcoal to deep brown, textured with fine crease patterns that become more pronounced with age.

The trunk extends from the upper face — a long, fully prehensile muscular organ of approximately 40,000 muscle fibers, capable of exerting significant force in any direction, of manipulation requiring extreme delicacy, and of serving as the primary sensory organ for olfaction. The trunk tip is the most sensitive structure in the Loxod body. Physical touch between trunk tips — offered between individuals who trust each other — is the most intimate form of Loxod contact.

The tusks are permanent keratin-and-ivory structures that grow throughout the Loxod lifespan. In younger adults they are modest weapons; in individuals over a century they are massive, curved, and deeply personal — growth patterns, wear marks, and repairs tell the physical history of a life. The tusks are never removed voluntarily. Removing them is mutilation, not tool use.

The ears are large and mobile, functioning for both hearing and thermal regulation. The feet retain the fatty seismic-sensing pad structure of their elephant ancestors — Loxod literally feel infrasound through the ground, receiving signals from others of their kind at significant distances even when those signals are inaudible to any other species present.

The olfactory system is exceptional in capacity. Their olfactory receptor gene count exceeds that of any other known species. The neurological pathway from the olfactory bulb to the hippocampal-amygdalar complex is direct, fast, and bidirectional in a way most species' systems are not. This means chemical inputs — particularly those associated with high-intensity emotional or physiological states — do not require cortical processing before generating memory and emotional response. They arrive already experienced. The Loxod cannot choose to process the smell of death intellectually first. The emotional and memorial response precedes the thought.

**Physical baseline:**
- Height: 2.50 – 3.00 m
- Weight: 400 – 500 kg
- Lifespan: 200 – 300 years
- Size category: Large

**Biological sex and reproduction:**
Loxod society has historically been matriarchal — the oldest female in any group historically held navigational and decision-making authority. This biological tendency toward matriarchal organization persists in their social structures, though it is not mechanically determining. They reproduce normally, with lifespans and generational turnover that make institutional memory possible in ways unavailable to shorter-lived species.

**Variants:**
None. The Loxod have no biological caste system. Individual variation exists in trunk and tusk development, olfactory sensitivity, and infrasound emission range, but these are spectra within one population, not defined types.

## 3. Culture

Loxod culture is a system of controlled attenuation.

Their cities are not positioned for resources or politics in the way most settlements are. They are positioned for resonance management: in geological features that dampen seismic infrasound, away from Tauma-thin zones where their signal becomes a beacon, surrounded by architecture designed to disperse rather than concentrate. The surface appearance of the city tells you very little — most of the critical infrastructure is acoustic and chemical, invisible to anyone who doesn't know what they're looking at.

The archive is the central institution. Not a library in the conventional sense — a library holds external documents. A Loxod archive holds what members have absorbed and organized: the reconstructed experiences of places they've walked through, deaths they've been near, events they've involuntarily received. The process of archiving is also the process of safely externalizing what would otherwise continue to resonate internally. Writing something down, encoding it properly, placing it in the institutional record — this is how an individual Loxod processes an impression enough that it stops vibrating continuously in their nervous system. The archive is therapy before it is scholarship.

Knowledge in Loxod society is not simply information. It is material with resonance properties that must be handled correctly. A piece of knowledge about how something catastrophic happened carries some of the frequency of the catastrophe. How it is stored, transmitted, who is allowed to receive it, in what form and context — these are not security decisions, they are acoustic decisions. Spreading certain knowledge incorrectly does not simply create an information hazard. It creates a resonance event in the receiver.

Hierarchy in Loxod society is defined by what a person can safely carry. The highest-ranking individuals are those who have absorbed the most without breaking — who have processed catastrophic impressions, organized them, and can continue to function. This is not a hierarchy of privilege. It is a hierarchy of capacity. The person at the top of a Loxod institution is not necessarily powerful in the political sense; they are simply the person who has demonstrated they can hold the most without losing themselves. The expectation is that they will carry what others cannot.

## 4. Theology and Cosmology

The Loxod understand the Tauma empirically before they understand it theologically.

They discovered its existence the same way early scientists discover most things: by noticing that behavior they couldn't explain had a pattern, and that the pattern implied a substrate. The infrasound channels they use for communication produce anomalous returns under certain conditions — their own signal comes back having passed through environments with vestige density, and the vestige's crystallized pattern modulates the return. The result is a signal that appears to carry content no living Loxod produced — content whose shape belongs to communities that no longer exist. The resonance events that occur when their cities sit too close to Tauma-active zones are documentable and repeatable. Over centuries of archival practice, the Loxod have accumulated more direct empirical data about the Tauma's behavior in specific conditions than any other species except perhaps those who work directly in taumaturgic traditions.

What the archive has established is that these anomalous returns fall into two categories. The first: returns with fixed shape. Documented repeatedly across generations, they produce the same modulation. They can be archived, compared, cross-referenced. They are the crystallized record of what happened in those spaces. The second category does not fix. Returns in this category vary between observations in ways that pattern analysis cannot predict. The most experienced archivists describe them as signals that seem to respond to the observer rather than simply arriving. The first category has working names. The second does not — only the accumulated institutional consensus that it must not be treated as the same thing.

What they have not done is build a cosmology around it. They have a working model. The working model is: the universe operates at certain fundamental frequencies; the Tauma is the texture of the universe at those frequencies; living things that exist in the universe share some degree of resonance with that texture; the Loxod share more than most, specifically in their infrasound biology; the consequences of this are manageable if managed and catastrophic if ignored.

Theology, for the Loxod, is the study of what manages it best. Their "religious" practices are indistinguishable from their archival practices and their acoustic architecture — because to the Loxod these things are all the same practice. The ritual of correctly encoding a received impression before it causes a resonance cascade in the encoder is, functionally, both a sanitation protocol and a devotional practice.

Their specific civilizational anxiety: **they have accumulated more direct knowledge about the Tauma than they can ever fully organize, and they are not certain whether they are containing it or whether it is, slowly, containing them.**

## 5. Organization and Internal Variation

The fundamental unit of Loxod social organization is the **archive collective** — a group of individuals bound not by kinship but by what they've collectively received and are jointly responsible for holding. The archive collective manages a shared resonance environment: what impressions its members have absorbed, how those impressions interact, whether any single member is approaching the threshold of what they can carry without destabilizing. The collective is both administrative unit and early warning system.

Leadership of a collective is informal but clear in practice: whoever has demonstrated capacity to hold more is deferred to in decisions about what to receive, when, and in what context. This leader does not govern individuals' lives — they govern the collective's relationship with incoming information.

Loxod who leave their collective — either by choice or necessity — are considered structurally vulnerable. The collective's function is partly to distribute the resonance load; a single Loxod absorbing everything they encounter without shared processing is, eventually, going to break. This is not dramatic or sudden. It is a slow accumulation. The old ones who have watched it happen describe it as a gradual displacement: the impressions become louder than the person's own experience, and at some point the Loxod begins to have difficulty distinguishing which memories are theirs.

**Loxod in the wider world** are rare. Their presence in non-Loxod settlements is typically purposeful: they came for something specific, they are managing something that required travel, they are in a period of voluntary isolation for reasons their collective approved. They do not explain their biology to most people they meet because the explanation is long and most species do not have the conceptual framework to receive it correctly.

## 6. Daily Life and Life Cycle

The Loxod lifespan is long enough that an individual can reasonably expect to outlive most institutions they encounter. This shapes how they relate to time: short-term urgency rarely reads to them the way it reads to shorter-lived species. A problem that must be solved in the next decade is a medium-term problem, not an emergency. The institutional memories they carry are often direct recollections, not historical records — an elder Loxod describing events from two centuries ago is not recounting history, they are remembering.

Young Loxod spend their first decades learning to attenuate. The olfactory system is at full sensitivity from early life; the discipline of managing incoming impressions, of not being overwhelmed by chemical information from every environment you enter, is the primary developmental task of Loxod childhood. Elders who work with children are doing what is most critical and least visible in Loxod education: teaching the practice of organized reception, so that what comes in can be held without breaking.

The passage into full adult status in Loxod society is not age-defined. It is capacity-defined: when a Loxod has demonstrated that they can receive a catastrophic impression, encode it correctly, and continue to function, they are considered an adult. Some reach this point at forty; some not until eighty. There is no shame in a longer developmental path — it is simply more time before you can be trusted with the archive's heavier materials.

Death for a Loxod is a complex archival event. The impressions an elder carries are not retrievable from the exterior after death — they die with the individual. Before death, elders spend significant time in formal archival sessions, transmitting what they've held to other members of the collective. An elder who dies suddenly, before transmission, is a genuine loss of irreplaceable held experience. The grief is real and also practical.

## 7. External Relations

Other species find the Loxod difficult to read and sometimes find themselves feeling that difficulty as a vague unease they can't source. The Loxod's size, composure, and the fact that they appear to be thinking several things simultaneously produce an impression of inscrutability that is partly accurate. They are often thinking several things simultaneously. Their olfactory reception of a social environment — the stress hormones, the chemistry of deception or conflict or attraction — is not available to the other party in the conversation. The Loxod knows more about what is happening physiologically in a room than most of the room knows about itself.

They do not typically leverage this asymmetry. The discipline of not acting on involuntarily received information without the other party's knowledge is considered basic ethical conduct in Loxod culture — related to the deeper principle that received information must be handled correctly or it creates harm. A Loxod who uses olfactorily absorbed emotional information to manipulate someone without disclosure is doing something their culture considers genuinely dishonorable, not merely impolite.

Species that have had extended contact with Loxod tend to describe them as the most reliably patient beings they've encountered, and also as the ones most capable of ending a relationship permanently without apparent anger. The patience comes from genuine long-term orientation. The clean endings come from the same place: when a Loxod decides something is no longer worth the resonance it creates, that decision does not reverse.

**To the Naghii:** The Naghii respect Loxod archives, but do not always understand their nature. For a Naghii, the archive preserves a chain of interpretation. For a Loxod, the archive also discharges a pressure that would otherwise remain inside someone. Both peoples fear transmitting badly. But the Naghii fear incorrect translation above all; the Loxod fear formless reception.

**To the Sauri:** The Sauri share with the Loxod the idea that certain forces must be contained by structure, body, and space. However, the Loxod usually distrust the Sauri's willingness to preserve bodies, sovereigns, or charged chambers for too long. For a Sauri, containing can be victory. For a Loxod, containing without discharge can simply be a slow form of contamination.

**To the Zarnag:** The Zarnag work with what is left after death; the Loxod receive what death leaves in the air. This can produce deep cooperation in disaster sites. The Zarnag knows what must be closed in earth, flesh, water, and Remnant. The Loxod knows what continues to resonate in those who enter. It can also produce tension: the Zarnag distrust any people who listen too closely to the dead without getting their hands dirty, and the Loxod fear that the Zarnag habit of resisting horror normalizes burdens that should be processed.

**To the Drak'kai:** The Drak'kai attract Loxod caution because of their collections. An object carried for decades might have accumulated impressions its owner does not fully perceive. The Drak'kai value the finding, the route, and the permanence. The Loxod ask what is resonating inside the object and what it will do when someone receives it unprepared.

**To the Rokhart:** The Rokhart produce a complex intellectual relationship. The Concord observes, records, and avoids intervening. The Loxod record because not recording can destroy the receiver. Both value method, but the emotional origin of the archive is different. A Rokhart might consider a Loxod too close to the data. A Loxod might reply that some data only exists because someone was willing to receive it in their body.

**To the Formix:** The Formix pose a difficult question about agency to the Loxod. The residual obedience chemistry, the Queen's Signal, and the bodily marks of function reach the Loxod body as intense information. Free Formix can feel invaded by a species that perceives too much of what they are trying to separate from themselves. The most careful Loxod communities treat Formix with strict protocols of perceptual consent.

**To the Panin:** The Panin are almost the opposite of the Loxod in their experience of time. Where the Loxod carry impressions for decades, the Panin live in the present signal. That difference can produce frustration, but also relief. A Panin can enter a charged situation and act before being trapped by the historical weight. A Loxod can prevent that action from ignoring what the place is still saying.

**To the Myo:** The Myo are listened to with attention and fear. Their oracles can name patterns the Loxod have felt for years without being able to convert into safe language. But the Loxod distrust any transmission that arrives without the proper discharge process. An oracular truth might be correct. It can also be unbearable for whoever receives it without structure.

**To the Ceratox:** The Ceratox share the experience of living with a body that constantly processes the environment. The difference lies in the type of signal and its social management. The Ceratox calibrate. The Loxod encode and discharge. Both can understand the cost of not being able to completely close off perception.

**To the Arakhel:** The Arakhel inhabit crossings where memory, passage, offering, and transit accumulate without becoming an archive in the Loxod sense. For a Loxod, an Arakhel node can resonate with too many layers at once: who crossed, who promised, who returned, who couldn't return. The Arakhel usually remind them that not everything a place guards must be discharged into a body. The Loxod usually reply that not everything a place guards knows when to stop speaking.

**To the Bufoni:** The Bufoni work with domains that recognize, respond, and remember territorially. To the Loxod, that can seem like a form of living archive distributed in soil, water, offering, and custom. To the Bufoni, the Loxod gaze can be dangerous if it tries to receive too much from a domain without belonging to its protocol. Both understand that a place can carry memory. They disagree on who has the right to receive it.

**To the Ursari:** The Ursari understand the body transformed by prolonged craft. A forge, like a Loxod archive, can be a place where matter, memory, and work leave a mark. The Loxod respect the Ursari who know when a work weighs too heavily on the artisan. They distrust those who call mastery what might already be distortion. The Ursari, for their part, might consider that the Loxod encode too much of what must sometimes be resolved by striking, melting, or transforming.

**To the Chelicer:** The Chelicer produce a clear ethical tension. For a Chelicer, certain truths are revealed under trial, risk, and procedure. For a Loxod, a truth revealed carelessly can displace the receiver who receives it. The Chelicer ask if someone is willing to endure the verdict. The Loxod ask if that body will still be theirs afterward.

**To the Luphran:** The Luphran preserve memory in crew, route, jarl, song, and living ship. That interests the Loxod because not all Luphran memory is written or isolated in an archive; it lives in coordination, loyalty, and shared repetition. A Loxod can feel the resonance of a crew before understanding its history. A Luphran might distrust someone who receives too much from the ship or the dead without having sworn loyalty aboard.

For many species, the Loxod are memory, patience, and judgment. For the Loxod, that reputation is incomplete.
Memory is not a virtue if it is not processed. Patience is not kindness if it only delays a necessary decision. And judgment should not belong to whoever received more than they could carry.

## 8. Material Expression and Language

**Loxian** — the Loxod language operates simultaneously on two channels: spoken sound (audible, structured, used for precision) and infrasound vibration (subaudible, continuous, carrying emotional and contextual information that the spoken layer cannot fully encode). To speak Loxian as a non-Loxod is to operate on only the surface channel. You understand the words but miss everything the vibration carries. A Loxod who is speaking to you is having a richer conversation than the one you're receiving. Among themselves, Loxian can communicate meaning that has no equivalent in other languages — emotional states that require a dozen words in any other tongue arrive as a single felt vibration.

**Architecture** in Loxod cities prioritizes acoustic and olfactory management. The materials are selected for their dampening properties. The layouts disperse rather than concentrate resonant energy. From the outside, their cities can appear austere — there is no ornamentation that doesn't serve a function, and function means management, not display. The interiors of archives are the most carefully engineered spaces in the world: temperature-controlled, chemically neutral, with acoustic properties that allow reception and encoding with minimal interference.

**Visual art** in Loxod culture primarily encodes information rather than expresses it. Loxod calligraphy is among the most complex writing systems extant — not for its graphic beauty, though it has that, but because each written element carries structural meaning about how the information it contains should be received and processed. Reading Loxian text correctly requires knowing not just the language but the encoding discipline. A text read by someone without that discipline produces incomplete understanding at best and, in extreme cases, a resonance effect in the reader.

## 10. Player Character Hooks and Open Questions

**Why a Loxod leaves:**
An individual departure from a Loxod collective is structurally unusual but not impossible. Common reasons: a received impression points to something that requires direct investigation the collective cannot authorize; an elder has requested a specific action that requires presence elsewhere; the collective itself was destroyed or disrupted and the individual is managing alone until they can reestablish contact; voluntary isolation for a development phase that the collective approved but that requires extended travel.

A Loxod PC is not running away from something they couldn't handle. They are doing something that requires being away from the structure that would normally help them handle it.

**What they arrive with:**
The accumulated impressions of a long life and deep archival training. Chemical and seismic awareness of their environment that other party members simply do not have. The infrasound beacon that makes certain places significantly more dangerous for the group. Patience calibrated to centuries that makes short-term urgency sometimes genuinely difficult to match. The trunk, which is both tool and weapon and the thing other species find most disorienting to watch in use.

**What they need:**
A stable partner or group to functionally replace part of the processing support the collective would normally provide. Not because they are incapable without it — but because the impressions keep accumulating, and organized transmission helps. A Loxod who travels alone for years without anyone to transmit to is carrying a growing weight. The older they are, the heavier what they hold already was before they left.

### Canon Resolutivo: Base para el Módulo de Campaña

- **La Impronta Olfativa:** Sentir la muerte o el estrés residual de una habitación no es magia, ni es automático. Es el uso activo de su Legado *Olfato Profundo* (una tirada técnica). Lo leen químicamente, no taumáticamente.
- **La Incomprensión del Tauma:** Los Loxod no entienden ni perciben el Tauma. Lo que perciben son *consecuencias físicas*. Cuando su infrasonido rebota en un Vestigo o una Entidad, no ven "magia", ven una distorsión acústica que no debería estar ahí, como si chocara con un muro invisible. Todo su conocimiento "taumático" es empírico y ciego: estudian la sombra que arroja la anomalía en el mundo físico, no la anomalía en sí.
- **Supresión de Firma Sísmica:** Un Loxod no puede "apagar" su vibración biológica. Sin embargo, puede portar un Vestigo como el *Mandala Dhvani* que disipe físicamente esa resonancia hacia el exterior, anulando su penalización a Sigilo, pero como costo narrativo, "cegando" temporalmente su propia percepción de ecos.
- **Relación con los Naghii:** Los sacerdotes Naghii ven a los Loxod como blasfemos fascinantes. Los ven como criaturas que literalmente "sienten la huella de los dioses" en la tierra, pero en lugar de adorarla, tratan de aislarla en un laboratorio de sonido.

## 11. Cultura Material y Herramientas de Atenuación

La cultura material Loxod (inspirada en la India Védica y los Osarios de elefantes africanos) está diseñada casi exclusivamente para lidiar con el abrumador peso de la "sobrecarga" de memoria y su resonancia incontrolable con el mundo físico.

- **Mandala *Dhvani* (Diagrama de Resonancia):** Placas pesadas talladas con geometría sagrada. Las instalan a gran escala en sus ciudades para disipar las distorsiones acústicas del ambiente. Los aventureros a veces cargan versiones pequeñas (como Vestigos) para silenciar su firma sísmica en territorio hostil, sacrificando su radar natural.
- **El *Ankus* de Atenuación:** Un punzón ritual (análogo a la herramienta de los *mahouts*). No lo usan como arma para dañar, sino para tocar físicamente a un compañero en estado de "desplazamiento" o pánico químico, anclándolo de vuelta a la realidad presente.
- **Colmillo de Registro (Estilógrafo de Memoria):** Así como Ganesha rompió su colmillo para escribir la historia del mundo, los Loxod graban sus hallazgos más insoportables en fragmentos de marfil de sus ancestros. Codificar la memoria en el hueso es la única forma de vaciarla de su propia mente.
- **Osarios de Memoria:** Inspirado en los cementerios de elefantes africanos. Ciertas cuencas geográficas acústicamente neutras donde los ancianos Loxod van a morir. Dejan sus huesos grabados física y químicamente con la historia de siglos, sirviendo como los archivos definitivos para que las futuras generaciones los lean olfativamente.
