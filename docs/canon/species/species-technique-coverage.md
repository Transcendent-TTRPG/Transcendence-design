# Species Technique Coverage

**Status:** Working reference  
**Scope:** Current authored species-origin coverage across Sauri and Naghii  
**Purpose:** Help future species passes preserve thematic fit while also
spreading authored coverage across Weapon Technique Profiles and
specializations.

---

## Design Rule

When starting a new species pass:

1. begin from what is thematically and dynamically true for the species
2. check the current authored coverage map
3. prefer underused profiles and specializations when they are a real match
4. repeat an already-used surface only when it is clearly the best fit

This document is not a quota engine. It is a balancing lens for species-origin
authoring.

- `Perforation` and `Interruption` are currently the most established authored

  dominant.
- `Corrosion`, `Unpredictability`, `Precision`, `Ricochet`, `Volley`,
  `Skirmish`, `Torsion`, `Ward`, `Rend`, and `Unstoppable` are all still light.
- `Charge`, `Shadow Pressure`, `Sunder`, `Flow`, `Lethality`, and `Deflection`
  have not yet appeared in finished species-origin Techniques at all.

This means a new species should not chase missing profiles blindly, but if the
species truly supports any of those underused or unused profiles, they are
especially valuable places to author next.

## Authoring Guidance For New Species

### Good questions

- What does this species *actually* do under pressure that others do not?
- Which profiles express its body logic or combat philosophy best?
- Which specializations emerge from daily labor, not just combat aesthetics?
- Which underused surfaces are a real fit rather than a coverage trick?

### Bad questions

- What profile has not appeared yet, regardless of species fit?
- Which specialization can be forced in just to fill a gap?
- How do we avoid every repetition, even when repetition is the most truthful choice?

### Healthy outcome

The ideal result is not perfect evenness.

The ideal result is:

- believable species identity
- broad long-term system coverage
- slower concentration in only a few dominant surfaces

Repeated use is acceptable when it is the clearest thematic answer. The
coverage map exists to stop lazy repetition, not to ban recurrence.
