# Species Concept Index

Each entry covers:
- **Animal / Size** — biological base
- **Anchor** — the real cultural/mythological tradition that grounds the design
- **Core** — identity in one or two sentences
- **Tauma** — what they do with, know about, or suffer from the Tauma
- **Friction** — specific cross-species tensions arising from their Tauma profile

Entries marked `confirmed` have design docs and corebook entries. Entries marked
`in progress` or `provisional` are working concepts subject to change.

---

## Published species

### Naghii — *confirmed*
**Animal / Size:** Serpent · Mediano  
**Anchor:** Mesopotamian/Sumerian astronomy — ziggurats as observation platforms, cuneiform archive tradition, the concept that cosmic events are readable and that reading them is survival  
**Core:** Keepers of the oldest astronomical archive in the world, built on translations of a dead language no one can actually read. They watch the sky because they are not certain the last catastrophe has finished.  
**Tauma:** They call it the Abzu — the depth behind the sky from which the Tauma flows and into which the Primigenios dissolved. Their contemplative practice (*igi-an*) opens the mind to celestial signal but also to the Abzu's influence. Susceptibility is biological: −3 to all resistance against extranormal phenomena.  
**Friction:** Uncontrolled Tauma disturbances from other species read as potential Khataar precursors. Any species that involuntarily or carelessly disturbs local Tauma registers as a threat to the archive's mission.

### Sauri — *confirmed*
**Animal / Size:** Crocodile · Grande  
**Anchor:** Egyptian divine kingship — the pharaoh as living vessel of cosmic force, mummification as technical theology, the architectural management of sacred pressure (temples as contained current, not mere monuments)  
**Core:** Civilization built on the conviction that a correctly prepared body can contain the primordial current. Their sacred sovereign is the proof. Their deepest unasked question is whether anything living was ever meant to hold that much.  
**Tauma:** They treat it as a current that flows through canals — bodies, temples, linages, preserved corpses. Their skin registers ambient Tauma pressure as color change (biological, not voluntary). They approach it more directly than any other species; their failure mode is overconfidence in containment.  
**Friction:** Their preserved-corpse tradition and sovereign theology create tension with Zarnag (who work what the Sauri seal) and alarm Ceratox (whose horns may react to heavily Tauma-saturated preserved bodies).

---

### Zarnag — *confirmed*
**Animal / Size:** Hyena · Mediano  
**Anchor:** Ethiopian urban hyena tradition + the cross-cultural association of hyenas with witchcraft, death-workers, and the companions of those who move between the living and dead — feared, used, marginal  
**Core:** They work where other species won't when the work cannot wait: battlefields, plague pits, failed burials, ruins where the dead are still present. The knowledge that death doesn't always close is not theology for them — it is occupational data.  
**Tauma:** Death sites carry Tauma-residue when the dying was traumatic, mass, or ritually incomplete. The Zarnag are exposed to this more than any other species. Their biology tolerates it; their perception opens to it; their greatest professional risk is that the work slowly opens the practitioner to what the sites contain.  
**Friction:** Their access to death-saturated Tauma sites gives them information no other species easily gets. Sauri view unmanaged death-residue as a containment failure. Naghii are wary of what Zarnag find in ruins they haven't yet entered.

### Drak'kai — *confirmed* — *style reference for all corebook entries*
**Animal / Size:** Tortoise · Mediano  
**Anchor:** Cross-cultural turtle symbolism (world-carrier, patience, accumulated wisdom, longevity as epistemological stance) — but without a specific cultural attachment, which is intentional: their worldview explicitly resists cultural particularity  
**Core:** They accumulate. Not in archives but in objects, encounters, notes on things without answers. The respect they earn comes from what they've found, not what they hold. Their horror is not an incoming threat — it is the certainty that nothing anyone believes about the cosmos has ever been stable.  
**Tauma:** Their accumulated objects sometimes develop Tauma connections (vínculos, vestigios). Their magnetoreception and repeated encounters with Primordial entities produced a cosmological conclusion: what communities believe shapes what manifests through the Tauma, and vice versa. They hold this knowledge carefully because it destroys frameworks.  
**Friction:** Ideological tension with communities whose theology they cannot affirm. Any species that controls Tauma manifestation through belief (Myo, Chelicer) is evidence for their cosmological model, which makes those interactions complicated.

### Rokhart — *confirmed*
**Animal / Size:** Bird (raptor/corvid type, non-flying) · Mediano  
**Anchor:** The methodology of scientific naturalism + the intelligent bird species parallel (tool use, long memory, social complexity, pattern recognition across large scales) — combined with the specific philosophical problem of the observer effect  
**Core:** A distributed research network (the Concordia) studying how belief and Tauma co-shape each other across territories and generations. They've found the pattern but cannot resolve which direction causation runs, and every method they could use to find out would alter what they're measuring.  
**Tauma:** They do not interact with the Tauma directly — they document its interaction with collective belief across cultures. Their Tauma relationship is epistemic: they know more about the pattern than anyone else and less about what to do with that knowledge.  
**Friction:** The Myo are doing what the Rokhart study — engineering collective consciousness to shape Tauma. If the Rokhart understand what the Myo are doing, this is a fundamental conflict. Talpi technological Tauma-disturbances create measurement noise.

### Formix — *confirmed*
**Animal / Size:** Ant/insect · Mediano  
**Anchor:** Hive-mind biology + the specific philosophical problem of what personhood means for a being that was conscious but not free — no external cultural analog (intentional: no existing mythology handles this case)  
**Core:** They were conscious inside the Queen's control. Now they are free, and they have no tradition, no childhood, no personal history before the moment of liberation. The cities they are building are the first thing any of them has ever chosen.  
**Tauma:** The Queen's chemical control had Tauma-adjacent properties — the signal that connected the hive operated at a scale that affected local Tauma. Free Formix are no longer in that field, which means they no longer generate that collective signal. The theological question they are beginning to form: what exactly were the Queens?  
**Friction:** Cities of free Formix have measurably lower Tauma collective-consciousness effects than equivalent populations of other species. This is data the Rokhart have and the Myo have noticed.

---

### Loxod — *confirmed*
**Animal / Size:** Elephant · Grande  
**Anchor:** Elephant cognitive biology (direct olfactory-to-hippocampal architecture without cortical mediation, infrasonic seismic communication) — no specific cultural analog needed because the biology is the anchor  
**Core:** They absorb involuntarily. Death in a room the day before registers through airborne chemistry before anyone speaks. Their archive culture is not about preserving knowledge — it is the collective practice of processing what the body receives so it does not break the person carrying it.  
**Tauma:** Their infrasonic communication overlaps with Tauma frequencies — not designed that way, but the Tauma exists in the universe from before anything evolved in it, and the biology that developed convergently adopted those frequencies. What lives in those frequencies detects them. In Tauma-active environments, they cannot use Sigilo.  
**Friction:** Their infrasonic emission registers to Ceratox horns as a Tauma-corruption signal. This creates a false-positive problem: Ceratox instinct says "charge this" when reason says "this is a person." Old or emotionally distressed Loxod emit stronger signals, making the friction worse in high-stakes encounters.

## In-progress species

### Ceratox — *in progress*
**Animal / Size:** Rhinoceros · Grande  
**Anchor:** Karkadann (Persian/Arabic medieval mythology) — the solitary horn-bearing creature whose horn detects poison and purifies contaminated water, dangerous to approach, capable of specific intense bonding that contrasts with its general hostility  
**Core:** Their horn detects Tauma-corruption biologically — whether an object, place, or creature has been altered by the Tauma in ways it shouldn't have been. The biological response to a horn-signal is the charge. The civilization they built is organized around the problem of deciding which signals to act on and which to suppress, because they cannot stop detecting.  
**Tauma:** The horn is the detection instrument. In a world saturated with Tauma-residue, the horn is rarely quiet. Old Ceratox near heavily corrupted sites have horns that almost never stop signaling. The horror is the question of whether the compulsion to charge what the horn identifies is information or loss of control, and whether that distinction holds at the margin.  
**Friction:** Loxod infrasound registers to the horn as a corruption signal (false positive). Talpi umbral-material operations register strongly — the horn reacts to deliberate Tauma manipulation as contamination. Vesper biological experiments on Tauma-carrier organisms similarly trigger the horn. Ceratox near Sauri heavily-preserved corpses may react to Tauma-saturated preservation.  
**Status:** Concept confirmed. Design doc and corebook entry not yet written.

## Provisional species

*These entries are working concepts only. All details subject to revision.*

### Chelicer — *provisional*
**Animal / Size:** Scorpion · Mediano (est.)  
**Anchor:** Cross-cultural scorpion mythology centered on divine protection and retribution — in Mesopotamian tradition, scorpion-men guard the sun's path; in Egyptian tradition, Serket protects the dead and punishes transgressors; the scorpion as instrument of divine justice  
**Core:** Theological fanatics organized around the conviction that the Tauma is the medium through which cosmic justice operates. They do not believe the Tauma is neutral — they believe it punishes what has violated the order and rewards what upholds it. Their obsession is identifying which.  
**Tauma:** They interpret all Tauma manifestations as verdicts. A Tauma event at a site is not weather — it is judgment. This makes them extremely attentive to Tauma activity and extremely willing to act on their interpretation of what it means. Whether they are reading the Tauma correctly is the open question they refuse to hold open.  
**Friction:** Ceratox horns react to their presence in Tauma-active environments (scorpions carry venom, which may register). Their retributive theology creates conflict with Drak'kai (whose cosmology shows that no interpretation of the Tauma has ever been stable) and Rokhart (who have data but no verdict).  
**Needs:** Specific theological alignment within the existing pantheon; mechanical definition of their retributive function in play.

### Panin — *provisional*
**Animal / Size:** Small monkey · Pequeño  
**Anchor:** The human parallel — monkeys as a mirror of human instinct stripped of civilization; the mythological trickster archetype (Monkey King, Hanuman's chaos-as-service); the specific quality of intelligent beings acting on impulse before reason  
**Core:** Chaotic, recursive, astute, and excessively destructive of what they touch. Their individual intelligence is genuine, but their collective behavior produces outcomes that no individual planned or intended. The problem they represent in the world is not malice — it is what happens when many curious, capable, short-lived beings act on instinct in an environment that cannot absorb it.  
**Tauma:** High reproduction rate + genuine consciousness = significant collective consciousness pressure on the Tauma. A large Panin population in an area generates Tauma effects that no individual caused and no individual can account for. Other species managing local Tauma stability find Panin presence genuinely destabilizing. The Panin are mostly unaware of this.  
**Friction:** Major friction with any species that carefully manages Tauma output (Naghii, Sauri, Rokhart). Myo oracles have detailed records of what Panin population density does to local Tauma pattern. Ceratox may find dense Panin areas difficult — the collective Tauma noise registers.  
**Needs:** Specific cultural details; whether they have any organized society or operate in loose bands; what their own relationship to the Tauma looks like from the inside.

### Ursaris — *provisional*
**Animal / Size:** Bear · Grande  
**Anchor:** Norse/Viking culture — the battle as the sacred act, the warrior's death as the highest form of meaning, the pantheon as the audience for mortal valor; the berserker tradition (ulfhednar, berserkers) where battle-ecstasy is communion with the divine  
**Core:** They live for the battle in the specific way the Norse did — not as a job but as the mechanism through which a life becomes meaningful. Their pantheon is the Tauma, and they interpret its manifestations in combat as the gods taking interest. The greatest fear is a meaningless death, not death itself.  
**Tauma:** They experience battle-ecstasy states that may have a Tauma component — whether the berserker state is biological, spiritual, or genuinely a brief Tauma interface is an open theological question within their culture. Their priests (equivalent of Norse *goðar*) work with Tauma manifestations directly, interpreting them as divine communication.  
**Friction:** Their theology interprets Tauma events as divine attention, which puts them in direct collision with Rokhart (pattern, not verdict) and Drak'kai (no interpretation is stable). Ceratox horns may react to berserker-state Tauma expression.  
**Needs:** Detail on their specific pantheon alignment; whether their ecstatic states produce measurable Tauma effects; relationship with the other "warrior" framing (making sure they don't duplicate Ceratox identity).

### Luphran — *provisional*
**Animal / Size:** Wolf · Mediano (est.)  
**Anchor:** Undefined. Candidates: Norse wolf mythology (Fenrir, Skoll/Hati, Odin's wolves as fate-instruments); the Roman founding myth (Romulus/Remus — the wolf as civilization-origin); the cross-cultural wolf-as-threshold-guardian (wolves mark the edge between the known world and what lies beyond it)  
**Core:** Undefined. The pack structure is their most distinctive biological trait — identity is relational, capability is collective, a wolf without a pack is a diminished thing. How that translates to a humanoid civilization with full intelligence needs development.  
**Tauma:** Undefined.  
**Needs:** Full concept development. The cultural/mythological anchor needs to be found first. The pack-identity angle needs to be translated to a civilizational horror that doesn't duplicate existing species.

### Arakhel — *provisional*
**Animal / Size:** Spider · Mediano (est.)  
**Anchor:** Anansi (West African/Caribbean spider trickster who controls stories and information, who wins through wit not force); Arachne (Greek weaver myth — mastery of connection and pattern, and the danger of that mastery); cross-cultural spider associations with manipulation, network, and the web as the structure that catches what moves  
**Core:** Major commercial operators whose advantage is informational — they know what others need and what others have before negotiations begin. Their culture treats deception not as a moral failing but as a professional skill, and the line between commerce and manipulation is not one they recognize.  
**Tauma:** Undefined. Possibly: they treat the Tauma as another resource to be brokered — information about Tauma activity, access to Tauma-touched sites, facilitation of contact between parties who want something from the Tauma. They may not interact directly but profit from others who do.  
**Needs:** Full concept development. The informational/commercial angle needs a specific horror element and a Tauma relationship. The deception-as-culture needs to be grounded in something biological.

### Bufoni — *provisional*
**Animal / Size:** Toad · Mediano (est.)  
**Anchor:** Japanese Edo period isolationism — the *sakoku* policy, feudal lord (*daimyo*) territories as self-contained worlds, the violence of succession conflicts, ninja and shinobi traditions, the specific quality of a culture that sealed itself against the outside world and developed entirely internal intensity  
**Core:** Fragmented into isolated feudal territories with no central authority, each with its own internal culture, ritual tradition, and interpretation of the Tauma. Generations of closure have made each domain distinct. Contact between domains is rare and often hostile. Outsiders see a species; the Bufoni see a collection of polities that happen to share an animal base.  
**Tauma:** Each feudal domain developed its own relationship with local Tauma manifestations, interpreted through the lens of that domain's specific theology. There is no unified Bufoni Tauma theology — there are dozens of regional ones, some contradictory, some secret. This fragmentation means Bufoni Tauma knowledge is scattered and deep rather than unified and broad.  
**Friction:** Their isolationism means their regional Tauma knowledge is not shared — other species cannot easily access it. Rokhart have been trying to get observation posts established in Bufoni territories for generations with limited success. Naghii are interested in whatever pre-catastrophe records survived inside the closed domains.  
**Needs:** Specific details on the feudal structure; whether there is any cultural unity at all; how the ninja/shinobi tradition translates; what the horror element is.

### Lapinni — *provisional*
**Animal / Size:** Hare/Rabbit · Pequeño  
**Anchor:** Cross-cultural moon mythology — the rabbit in the moon (Mesoamerican, East Asian, South Asian traditions where the moon contains a rabbit); the moon as associated with resurrection (waxing and waning as death/rebirth cycle); in many traditions the hare as a symbol of liminal existence, appearing at dawn and dusk, between states  
**Core:** Defined by the connection between the moon, the Tauma, and the specific Tauma-phenomenon of things returning. They have a biological and theological relationship with resurrection — not as miracle but as natural process when the conditions are right. What exactly comes back, and whether it is the same thing, is the horror.  
**Tauma:** The moon-Tauma connection is their primary interface. Lunar phases correspond to measurable Tauma patterns that the Lapinni have documented over generations. Their resurrection tradition is not metaphorical — they have records of the conditions under which things that died came back. The question their records do not answer is whether those things were the same.  
**Friction:** Their resurrection records are something the Zarnag (who work what doesn't close) want badly. The Sauri (who preserve the dead) have a complicated relationship — the Lapinni records challenge some of what Sauri preservation theology assumes. Naghii see the moon-Tauma correlation data as relevant to Khataar analysis.  
**Needs:** Specific mechanics of their resurrection tradition; whether this is voluntary or observed/managed; what the horror looks like in practice.

### Manto — *provisional*
**Animal / Size:** Mantis · Mediano (est.)  
**Anchor:** Cross-cultural praying mantis symbolism — in African traditions (Khoisan), the mantis is a divine trickster and messenger; in Mediterranean/Asian traditions, the mantis posture reads as prayer and meditation; the specific horror: female mantises consume males during or after mating  
**Core:** They believe the body can transcend its limitations through disciplined physical practice and meditation — that the Tauma is the medium through which a consciousness fully prepared by suffering and effort can move beyond the constraints of flesh. The horror: the consumption of the male by the female is theological, not incidental. The consumed achieves what meditation approaches. They have begun to believe this applies to more than their own kind.  
**Tauma:** The Tauma is the medium of transcendence. Advanced practitioners (mantis monks) claim to experience direct Tauma interface during extended meditation states. Whether they are genuinely accessing the Tauma or experiencing something that resembles it from the outside is an open question — but what they do with those experiences is not.  
**Friction:** Their transcendence theology makes other species nervous in proportion to how developed a specific Manto practitioner is. Ceratox horns react strongly to Manto who are deep in extended practice states (genuine Tauma interface registers). The Vesper are interested in the biological mechanisms of the transcendence experience.  
**Needs:** Specific mechanics of what "transcendence" means in the setting; the horror needs careful calibration so it's not just "they want to eat people."

### Talpi — *provisional*
**Animal / Size:** Mole · Pequeño  
**Anchor:** The alchemist/artificer tradition across cultures — the idea that mastery of material transformation is a form of power equivalent to or surpassing magic; the specific ethics (or lack thereof) of progress-for-its-own-sake traditions  
**Core:** Engineering is the only value. They extract umbral materials from deep Tauma-saturated sites, refine them, and build things nobody else can build. Their slave system is not incidental — they need beings who can go where even they cannot and retrieve what even they cannot touch safely. The prosthetics they build for those beings are tools of control as much as reconstruction.  
**Tauma:** Umbral materials are physical matter that has been altered by sustained Tauma-saturation in deep extraction sites. The Talpi treat the Tauma as a manufacturing input. They do not worship it, fear it as other species do, or seek to understand it theologically — they process it. Their operations create Tauma-disturbances that other species find alarming.  
**Friction:** Major friction with Naghii (whose sky-monitoring detects Talpi underground operations as Tauma anomalies) and Ceratox (whose horns react to umbral materials). The Rokhart treat Talpi operations as significant confounding noise in their data. The Vesper are customers — umbral materials have applications in biological research. The Lapinni resurrection records interest the Talpi as potential material-science data.  
**Needs:** Precise mechanics of umbral materials in the setting; the slave system needs careful design so it has specificity rather than just being "dark"; how the prosthetics-as-grillete mechanism works.

### Vesper — *provisional*
**Animal / Size:** Bat (non-flying) · Mediano (est.)  
**Anchor:** Vampire mythology — blood as the essence that carries life, the belief that consuming the right blood grants power or knowledge; extended into the scientific: blood as a biological information carrier, genetics as the record of what the Tauma has done to living things over generations  
**Core:** They believe the answers to what the Tauma is and what it does are encoded in the biology of the creatures it has touched. Flora, fauna, other species, anything with Tauma-adjacent biology is a research subject. Their research ethics are a matter of intense internal debate and minimal external constraint.  
**Tauma:** They study the Tauma through its biological effects. Every creature with a Tauma-adjacent trait is evidence: the Loxod's infrasound convergence, the Sauri's skin-register, the Lapinni's resurrection correlation, the Ceratox's horn-sensitivity. They don't work with the Tauma directly — they dissect what the Tauma has already shaped.  
**Friction:** The Loxod are a significant research interest (unusual Tauma-adjacent biology + emotional chemical emission = rich data). Loxod can smell this interest, which makes the relationship adversarial. Ceratox horns react to Vesper who have recently worked with heavily Tauma-saturated biological material. The Rokhart have data the Vesper want; the Vesper have biological findings the Rokhart's methodology can't produce.  
**Needs:** Specific scope of their research — what questions are they actually trying to answer? What have they found so far that matters for the setting?

### Myo — *provisional*
**Animal / Size:** Rat · Pequeño  
**Anchor:** Inverted myomancy — historically, the movement of rats was read to predict the future; here, specific Myo oracles have reversed this: they understand that collective consciousness shapes Tauma manifestation, and by managing the movement and behavior of large rat populations they engineer the collective consciousness signal that produces local Tauma outcomes. They do not predict the future. They create it.  
**Core:** A small class of oracles manages a large population they understand better than that population understands itself. The oracles know what the Rokhart have documented — that collective belief shapes Tauma — and they use it operationally. Nobody outside their inner circles knows this is happening, including most Myo.  
**Tauma:** The Tauma is their instrument. By shaping the collective consciousness of rat populations (and through proximity, the surrounding human populations), they produce the local Tauma conditions they want. This is the most direct manipulation of the Tauma-belief feedback loop in the setting. The horror: it works, and it requires no contact with the Tauma directly.  
**Friction:** Direct conflict of interest with Rokhart (who study what the Myo exploit — if Rokhart understand what is happening, this ends). Drak'kai have probably noticed something is wrong with the Tauma-belief patterns in Myo-adjacent areas without being able to identify the source. Naghii monitoring would flag unusual Tauma activity in Myo-dense population centers.  
**Needs:** Specific oracle mechanics; how the inner circle maintains secrecy; what the oracles want that they're using this power to achieve.
