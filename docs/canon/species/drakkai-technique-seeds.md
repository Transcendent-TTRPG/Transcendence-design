## Purpose

This document does not list finished Techniques.

Its purpose is to establish the structural foundation for Drak'kai technique authoring: which natural attack forms and weapon surfaces are available, which specializations are primary and secondary, and how the novice pass should be distributed.

Seed principles — the abstract conceptual seeds with `world_root`, `technique_space`, `authoring_pressure`, and `avoid` fields — are added iteratively with designer review after this skeleton is approved.

### Origin vs. access

**Drak'kai-origin techniques are not species-locked.** The species origin explains why a technique exists — what problem it was developed to solve, and what body of knowledge or practice produced it. The mechanical surface (weapon profile or specialization) determines who can execute it. Any character wielding a weapon with the required profile, or holding the required specialization, can use a Drak'kai-origin technique regardless of their species.

What the Drak'kai origin does: gives the technique its identity, constraints, and thematic coherence.
What it does not do: prevent non-Drak'kai characters from learning or executing it.

The test when authoring: *Could this technique exist without the Drak'kai?* If yes, the origin is too thin. *Could someone execute it with a different body, tool, or practice?* If no, the origin has become too species-locked. The target is the middle.

### What a Drak'kai Technique should be

A Drak'kai Technique should carry a recognizable method of solving a problem that emerged from Drak'kai body (weight, shell, crushing jaw, patient endurance), Drak'kai practice (long-route wandering, object handling, conclave exchange), and Drak'kai knowledge (accumulated encounters with the world and with things in the world that cannot be fully explained).

A Drak'kai Technique should not be a generic "wise elder" bonus, a generic heavy-armor fighter move, or a knowledge dump that bypasses the mechanical system.

The Drak'kai do not treat knowledge as decoration. They treat it as load-bearing — and the weight shows.

## Technique Lessons From The Species

The Drak'kai have three primary natural attack forms:

- **Mordisco / Bite — Principal:** `Impact`, `Unstoppable`, `Interception`, `Torsion`; committed jaw closure, hold sustained beyond initial contact, body weight behind the close — the bite is not a repeated tool, it is a final decision
- **Garras / Claws — Auxiliar:** `Line Control`, `Interception`, `Torsion`, `Interruption`; grip, control, space management, and supplementary close pressure — not raking aggression but handling and holding
- **Caparazón / Shell — Defensive:** `Bastion`, `Interception`, `Impact`, `Line Control`; structural reinforcement of the torso, body-anchor positioning, contact absorption — the shell is a defense surface, not an attack vector

They also have strong cultural and biological technique surfaces:

- maces, hammers, and blunt crushing weapons for committed heavy strikes
- heavy shields for anchor, barrier, and controlled passage denial
- polearms for approach management from a patient position
- Percepción and Rastreo for magnetoreceptive environmental reading and route history
- Supervivencia and Orientación for long-route endurance and wayfinding
- Intelecto specializations (Identificación, Historia, Arqueología, Geografía) for accumulated knowledge applied in specific moments
- Compostura specializations (Enfoque, Contención, Meditación) for patience, internal discipline, and sustained function under pressure
- object handling and vínculo/vestigio encounter practice as non-weapon Technique roots

Species-origin does not mean natural-weapon-only. A Drak'kai Technique may use the bite, shell, claws, mace, hammer, shield, polearm, accumulated knowledge, environmental reading, or patient preparation. What makes it Drak'kai is the principle underneath: the user solves a problem through commitment, structure, weight, and accumulated understanding — not through speed, deception, or volume of strikes.

## Novice Pass — Drak'kai Coverage Target

| Section | Target |
| --- | ---: |
| Attack / weapons | 12 |
| Defense | 2 |
| Resistance hybrids | 2 |
| Specializations | 8 |

### Attack / weapons — 12

The Drak'kai weapon pass should be planned at the profile level, not the item-family level. The identity is: commitment over volume, structure over speed.

| Profile | Baseline count | Likely expression surfaces | Why |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| `Impact` | 1 | Bite, mace, hammer, shield slam | Crushing contact — mass applied to a specific surface with full commitment. |
| `Unstoppable` | 1 | Bite, heavy mace | Once committed, the action continues through resistance — what they decide to close, stays closed. |
| `Interception` | 1 | Bite, claws, shield, polearm | Waiting, reading, then acting at the right moment — patience resolving into a specific interception. |
| `Torsion` | 1 | Bite (hold and turn), claws (grip redirect) | Once contact is made, redirecting force through the hold — the jaw or hand that changes angle. |
| `Line Control` | 1 | Claws, shield, shell positioning | Managing space and approach lines — not letting others flow around or through freely. |
| `Interruption` | 1 | Claws, polearm, shield | Spoiling an action through grip, reach, or structural placement. |
| `Bastion` | 1 | Shield, shell, heavy armor stance | Holding a position that others would not hold under the same pressure. |
| `Rend` | 1 | Bite (sustained), mace follow | Not the primary identity, but the bite's consequence when sustained long enough. |
| `Flow` | 0 | — | Drak'kai are not fluid fighters. This profile conflicts with their structural identity. |
| Dual-wield / mace + shield combination | 2 | Mace strike into shield anchor, or bite into claw grip | Two expressions of the same principle: close, anchor, continue. |
| Polearm reach and approach control | 2 | Polearm zone management, patient entry | Establishing distance before committing, then closing on their terms. |

### Defense — 2

| Surface | Count | Why |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Shell positioning and structure | 1 | One defense should use the shell's natural protection directly — body positioning to maximize structural absorption. |
| Patience-based defensive read | 1 | One defense should represent the Drak'kai advantage of reading an approach before it fully commits — not evasion, but early interception from a prepared position. |

### Resistance hybrids — 2

Resistance hybrids should use secondary specializations. Their job is to widen the species' resistance logic without stealing primary specialization slots.

| Surface | Count | Why |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `Contención` hybrid | 1 | Sustaining function under environmental pressure — enduring conditions that degrade others without losing the action. |
| `Enfoque` hybrid | 1 | Maintaining concentration under attack or distraction — the patient mind that keeps working when the body is under strain. |

### Specializations — 8 (primary)

| Specialization | Count | Why |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `Percepción` | 1 | Magnetoreceptive environmental reading — detecting what others miss in terrain, objects, and approach routes. |
| `Rastreo` | 1 | Route history and environmental tracking — reading what has moved through a space and how long ago. |
| `Orientación` | 1 | Navigation and wayfinding under conditions where landmarks don't suffice. |
| `Supervivencia` | 1 | Long-route endurance — managing resources, exposure, and function across distances and conditions. |
| `Enfoque` | 1 | Patient concentration — holding a task or position without degradation under mounting pressure. |
| `Contención` | 1 | Sustained function — enduring external or internal pressure without it affecting judgment or capacity. |
| `Identificación` | 1 | Reading objects, sites, and encounters through accumulated prior knowledge — recognizing what something is. |
| `Historia` | 1 | Applied accumulated context — knowing what is happening because you have seen its precedent before. |

### Secondary specialization pool

These are not part of the primary 8-Technique spine. They are available for resistance hybrids, deliberate swaps based on a specific Drak'kai community, and higher-rank expansion.

| Specialization | Secondary fit | Best use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Meditación` | Strong | Internal discipline, patience under extreme conditions, sustained self-regulation when the environment offers no support. |
| `Arqueología` | Strong | Reading ruined sites, identifying what something was before it became what it is now. |
| `Geografía` | Strong | Accumulated route knowledge at regional scale — where things are and why. |
| `Astronomía` | Moderate / strong by community | Long-route celestial navigation; also Drak'kai cosmological knowledge applied to specific readings. |
| `Lingüística` | Moderate | Accumulated language exposure from long travel; reading inscriptions, records, and fragments of the ancient world. |
| `Taumaturgia` | Moderate | Understanding the principles behind extranatural phenomena encountered through collection and study. |
| `Medicina` | Moderate | Long-route triage, wound management over distances, and the accumulated anatomical knowledge of a species that has seen many kinds of damage. |

### Novice pass profile pressure

Profiles that Drak'kai naturally help cover well:

- `Bastion`
- `Interception`
- `Impact`
- `Unstoppable`
- `Line Control`
- `Torsion`

Profiles Drak'kai might touch in smaller numbers:

- `Rend`
- `Interruption`

Profiles the novice pass should not force unless a concept genuinely demands them:

- `Flow`
- `Shadow Pressure`
- `Skirmish`
- `Unpredictability`
- `Charge`
- Ranged profiles

### Novice pass — initial authoring order

1. Establish the 12 attack/weapon profile assignments first
2. Start with bite and shell — they are the most structurally distinctive surfaces
3. Expand into maces/hammers and shields — committed blunt and anchor
4. Add polearm techniques only where the profile genuinely needs it
5. Write two defenses from shell positioning and patience-read
6. Write two resistance hybrids from `Contención` and `Enfoque` secondary surfaces
7. Close with the eight primary specialization Techniques
