## Purpose

This document does not list finished Techniques.

Its purpose is to define the Zarnag principles that can generate Techniques
later. A Zarnag Technique should not be a generic darkness power, a generic
poison strike, or a hyena-flavored intimidation bonus. It should carry a
recognizable method of solving a problem that emerged from Zarnag body, carrion
labor, grave management, plague survival, fear discipline, and the civilizational
burden of dealing with remains that do not stay harmless.

The Zarnag do not treat filth, death, or taboo as decorative themes. They
treat them as practical pressures that must be interpreted before they spread.

## Technique Lessons From The Species

The Zarnag have two primary natural attack forms:

- **Mordisco / Bite** - `Corrosion`, `Shadow Pressure`, `Rend`, `Charge`;
  infectious breach, panic at close range, tearing follow-through, and
  commitment once contact is made
- **Garras / Claws** - `Skirmish`, `Shadow Pressure`, `Rend`,
  `Unpredictability`; raking harassment, messy chained contact, opportunistic
  tearing, and pressure once the exchange turns ugly

They also have strong cultural combat surfaces:

- daggers and short blades for foul close work
- axes, cleavers, and work blades for stripping, opening, and harsh finishing
- thrown tools for warning, marking, or forcing distance around bad ground
- survival and tracking as route-reading in contaminated terrain
- medicine and anatomy as corpse judgment, triage, and infection discipline
- intimidation, poise, and social pressure through nerve, laughter, and taboo
- quarantine logic, burial correction, and scavenger-route management as
  non-weapon Technique roots

Species-origin does not mean natural-weapon-only. A Zarnag Technique may use a
bite, claws, knife, cleaver, hook, thrown implement, intimidation, corpse
handling, field medicine, burial labor, or camp discipline. What makes it
Zarnag is the principle underneath: the user solves danger by reading, managing,
or weaponizing the unstable remainder others refuse to touch.

## Seed Format

Each seed uses:

| Field | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `concept` | The abstract Zarnag principle |
| `world_root` | Why this principle exists in Zarnag society |
| `technique_space` | What kinds of Techniques this can generate |
| `possible_expressions` | Weapons, natural forms, competencies, or scene types that can express it |
| `transmission_logic` | How the principle is taught or restricted |
| `authoring_pressure` | What must remain true when writing a finished Technique |
| `avoid` | What would flatten the seed into generic mechanics |

## Zarnag Seed Principles

### 1. The Remainder Must Be Answered

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `concept` | What is left behind becomes dangerous if no one answers it. |
| `world_root` | Zarnag life is built around the fact that battlefields, plague pits, broken graves, and carcass routes do not resolve themselves. Their culture exists because remains unattended long enough become ecological, spiritual, and social threats. |
| `technique_space` | Techniques that stabilize a site, force a decision around remains, prevent a scene from worsening, protect a body from becoming a new hazard, or turn neglected residue into a tactical factor. |
| `possible_expressions` | Survival, Medicine, corpse handling, burial work, camp discipline, intimidation, escort scenes, post-combat control, route security. |
| `transmission_logic` | Common among any Zarnag community that works the dead; deeper forms taught by bone keepers, plague-front elders, and battlefield judges. |
| `authoring_pressure` | A finished Technique should define what remainder is being answered and what happens if no one deals with it. |
| `avoid` | Do not make this generic cleanup, healing, or lore flavor. The unresolved remainder must threaten the next state. |

### 2. Carrion Contact Changes The Exchange

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `concept` | Once foul contact is made, the fight is no longer clean. |
| `world_root` | Zarnag jaws, saliva, breath discipline, and corpse-side labor make them acutely aware that some forms of contact continue working after the first wound. Infection, residue, panic, and contamination all create consequences that outlast the initial hit. |
| `technique_space` | Bite Techniques that force resistance, create lingering impairment, punish delayed response, turn a wound into contamination risk, or make the target treat contact itself as the problem. |
| `possible_expressions` | Bite, short blades, dirty work knives, darts, contaminated surfaces, body-to-body struggle, plague scenes. |
| `transmission_logic` | Broadly understood, but disciplined forms are taught carefully because careless imitation spreads danger to allies as easily as enemies. |
| `authoring_pressure` | A finished Technique should care about what continues after contact: residue, panic, impairment, infection risk, or forced next-step response. |
| `avoid` | Do not make this flat poison damage or abstract decay magic. The danger should come from plausible hostile residue or contaminated breach. |

### 3. The Laugh Breaks Nerve First

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `concept` | Before a body breaks, certainty breaks. |
| `world_root` | Zarnag laughter is not comedy. It is fear discipline, pressure signaling, group hardening, and sometimes deliberate psychological abrasion. Communities that live near taboo labor learn quickly that morale collapses before flesh does. |
| `technique_space` | Techniques that create hesitation, spoil composure, force bad timing, turn disgust into pressure, push an enemy into panic, or steady allies through shared nerve under horrifying conditions. |
| `possible_expressions` | Intimidation, Poise, command under duress, bite follow-up, close harassment, social confrontation, corpse-field control. |
| `transmission_logic` | Informal and widespread, but formalized among fear breakers, trench wardens, and groups that must keep others moving in horrific environments. |
| `authoring_pressure` | A finished Technique should act on confidence, timing, or morale before it acts on raw harm. |
| `avoid` | Do not reduce this to generic loud intimidation. The pressure comes from someone remaining functional inside horror. |

### 4. The Scavenger Follows The Weak Line

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `concept` | The safest opening is the line already starting to fail. |
| `world_root` | Zarnag survive by reading failing burial walls, weak scavenger routes, soft ground, bad camp edges, and the first signs that a site is about to go wrong. That same logic shapes combat: they attack where order is already thinning. |
| `technique_space` | Techniques that punish instability, exploit disordered footing, chain contact once an enemy is off rhythm, follow a bad step, or deepen a collapse already beginning. |
| `possible_expressions` | Claws, daggers, axes, Tracking, Balance punishment, Skirmish, Unpredictability, field pursuit. |
| `transmission_logic` | Common among scouts, scavenger path readers, and close-fight practitioners who do not rely on clean duels. |
| `authoring_pressure` | A finished Technique should care about the line that is already weakening: footing, guard rhythm, morale, route stability, or body posture. |
| `avoid` | Do not make this generic bonus damage or random opportunism. The user is reading an actual weak line and worsening it. |

### 5. Quarantine Is Force With A Moral Cost

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `concept` | Some threats are stopped not by defeating them cleanly, but by isolating them before they spread. |
| `world_root` | Zarnag plague work, camp law, and carrion management all teach that delay can kill a whole community. A single body, trench, tool, or route may need to be cut off brutally for others to survive. |
| `technique_space` | Techniques that isolate a target, deny passage, hold a contaminated line, force spacing, pin a threat inside a bad route, or make approach itself dangerous. |
| `possible_expressions` | Interruption, Sunder, axes, shields, camp barriers, disease scenes, quarantine orders, late-hand spoil, and emergency no-touch enforcement. |
| `transmission_logic` | Taught in plague-front camps, border grave lines, and any Zarnag group responsible for others' survival under contamination pressure. |
| `authoring_pressure` | A finished Technique should preserve a living boundary or force a hard separation that someone may resent. |
| `avoid` | Do not make this generic crowd control. Quarantine is about containing spread, not just occupying space. |

### 6. Useful Filth, Forbidden Filth

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `concept` | Survival depends on knowing what can still be used and what must be burned, buried, or abandoned. |
| `world_root` | Zarnag subsistence and labor both depend on ruthless judgment about spoilage, residue, salvage, contamination, and taboo. Their expertise is not that they ignore filth; it is that they distinguish the survivable from the corrupting. |
| `technique_space` | Techniques that sort risk, identify bad material, salvage under danger, resist false safety, preserve a useful remainder, or expose something that only looks harmless. |
| `possible_expressions` | Medicine, Anatomy, Survival, Tracking, poison handling, ruin work, camp triage, post-battle salvage. |
| `transmission_logic` | Taught early in daily life, but sharpened into doctrine by renderers, carrion judges, and plague elders. |
| `authoring_pressure` | A finished Technique should make a distinction that matters: usable vs fatal, bearable vs spreading, recoverable vs cursed. |
| `avoid` | Do not make this generic appraisal or instant truth detection. The judgment should be materially grounded and costly if wrong. |

### 7. Better To Strike Before Rot Spreads

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `concept` | Delay can be more dangerous than commitment when degradation is already underway. |
| `world_root` | Zarnag communities constantly decide whether to wait, salvage, flee, burn, or cut something down before it worsens. This creates a combat logic of ugly but timely commitment: sometimes the correct answer is to go in before uncertainty multiplies. |
| `technique_space` | Techniques that combine entry with harsh interruption, rush a failing line, convert hesitation into forward pressure, or strike before a target stabilizes or spreads harm. |
| `possible_expressions` | Bite, axes, short blades, Charge profile, rough grappling, route breach, scavenger rush, intimidation into action. |
| `transmission_logic` | Less universal than fear discipline or corpse judgment; associated with battlefield followers, plague breakers, and hard-use hunting parties. |
| `authoring_pressure` | A finished Technique should leave the user committed because not acting was judged worse than the risk of acting. |
| `avoid` | Do not make this generic aggression or free movement burst. The point is desperate timing under worsening conditions. |

### 8. What The Dead Disturb Is Still Speaking

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `concept` | A site of death can be read through what it displaced, drew, spoiled, or silenced. |
| `world_root` | Zarnag are students of aftermath. They read insects, scent shifts, scavenger patterns, disturbed soil, exposed bone, missing carrion birds, foul runoff, and the behavior of animals around bad ground. |
| `technique_space` | Techniques that read hostile sites, predict danger from traces, detect reopening graves or disturbed remains, follow predator routes, or interpret what pressure a death-site is exerting on the living field around it. |
| `possible_expressions` | Tracking, Survival, Perception-like reading, corpse-field navigation, pursuit, ruin scouting, camp security. |
| `transmission_logic` | Broadly practical, then refined by grave walkers and route readers whose job is to arrive before the worst consequence does. |
| `authoring_pressure` | A finished Technique should identify the trace being read and the danger it implies. |
| `avoid` | Do not make this supernatural omniscience. The site is speaking through evidence, not revelation. |

### 9. The Necessary Hand Must Stay Steady

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `concept` | Someone must keep working while others recoil. |
| `world_root` | Zarnag labor often happens while others panic, gag, pray badly, flee, or argue. Their communities prize the person who can keep sorting, cutting, carrying, sealing, or deciding while everyone else wants distance. |
| `technique_space` | Techniques that hold composure under disgust, keep function through horror, let the user continue work or combat while others are destabilized, or make repulsive conditions less tactically disruptive. |
| `possible_expressions` | Poise, Tolerance, Medicine, camp labor, battlefield rescue, contamination handling, pressure support. |
| `transmission_logic` | Socially central and taught from youth, but hardened forms belong to plague camps, corpse teams, and those trusted with children or the wounded under bad conditions. |
| `authoring_pressure` | A finished Technique should preserve action under conditions that should be hard to remain calm inside. |
| `avoid` | Do not make this blank immunity to fear, disgust, or pain. The steadiness should protect a job, route, or duty. |

### 10. Waste Draws Predators

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `concept` | Careless aftermath creates new enemies. |
| `world_root` | Zarnag know that spilled blood, exposed meat, bad disposal, abandoned tools, and careless remains all alter the field after the fight. What a group leaves behind may summon monsters, scavengers, sickness, or enemies who read the sign correctly. |
| `technique_space` | Techniques that punish careless overkill, manage aftermath, erase useful traces, trap pursuers through residue logic, or turn what was left behind into a deliberate warning or lure. |
| `possible_expressions` | Survival, Tracking, thrown markers, trap work, corpse management, retreat doctrine, scavenger-route manipulation. |
| `transmission_logic` | Learned through survival and reinforced by every community that has suffered because someone left too much behind. |
| `authoring_pressure` | A finished Technique should care about what remains on the field after the action ends. |
| `avoid` | Do not make this abstract “resource efficiency.” The waste should have ecological or tactical consequences. |

## Converting A Seed Into A Technique

When writing a finished Technique from one of these seeds, use this order:

1. Choose the seed principle.
2. Choose the expression: bite, claws, knife, axe, thrown tool, specialization,
   social pressure, camp law, corpse-field labor, or contamination scene.
3. Define the playable situation.
4. Define the primary system surface.
5. Add only one secondary surface if the fiction truly asks for it.
6. Set the cost from timing, strain, and moral or bodily commitment.
7. Write restrictions that preserve the seed's identity.

The test is:

> Could this Technique exist without the Zarnag?

If yes, the origin is too thin.

> Could someone learn it from the Zarnag and perform it with a different body,
> tool, or scene practice?

If no, the origin has become too species-locked.

The target is the middle: a Technique that carries Zarnag thought into action.

### Zarnag Novice Pass - Proposed Distribution

The Zarnag novice pass should begin from the species' strongest practical
surfaces:

- foul close contact
- ugly short-range pressure
- weak-line exploitation
- quarantine and route denial
- aftermath reading
- staying functional inside disgust, contamination, and social panic

The pass should therefore avoid overcommitting to elegant duel logic,
formalized soldier geometry, or abstract “darkness” motifs.

#### Attack / weapons — 12 species-origin + 2 provisional shared fieldcraft techniques

The Zarnag weapon pass should be planned **at the profile level**, not first at
the item-family level.

Each profile used in the novice pass should normally receive **one authored
Technique**, unless a second Technique is clearly needed because that profile
expresses two very different Zarnag truths. The goal is to avoid overfilling one
surface just because multiple weapons can carry it.

| Profile | Baseline count | Likely expression surfaces | Why |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| `Corrosion` | 1 | Bite, dirty short blade, contaminated dart | Contact that keeps worsening the exchange after the wound. |
| `Shadow Pressure` | 1 | Bite, claws, dagger, laughter-adjacent close pressure | Panic, disgust, and invasive threat that destabilize confidence before clean control is restored. |
| `Rend` | 1 | Bite, claws, axe, short blade | Tearing, opening, and deepening a line already turning ugly. |
| `Charge` | 1 | Bite, rough rush, cleaver-led breach | Going in before the bad situation spreads further. |
| `Skirmish` | 1 | Claws, short blades | Harassment, weak-line pressure, and contact that refuses formal duel geometry. |
| `Unpredictability` | 1 | Claws, daggers, false-route pressure | Dirty angle theft, ugly chained contact, and wrong-read creation under stress. |
| `Lethality` | 1 | Short blades, finishing knife | Close finishing logic when hesitation is already fatal. |
| `Sunder` | 1 | Axe, cleaver, stripping tool | Breaking weak structure, splitting cover, or making a barrier stop functioning safely. |
| `Interruption` | 1 | Hook, close spoil, late-hand catch, no-touch enforcement | Quarantine logic reads more naturally here than in live route-marking. Zarnag can support one strong reactive spoil without drifting into trap setup. |
| `Precision` | 1 | Thrown warning tool, exact placement strike, useful-piece disruption | Not sniper logic, but exact one-target placement when the wrong piece, strap, latch, or exposed implement has to be hit now. |
| `Dual-wield close sequence` | 2 | Claw + dagger, dagger + hooked sidearm, paired short blades | One expression can simply be a faster same-body two-hand finish; the second can turn the pair into exit pressure or crowding control. This suits Zarnag close brutality better than keeping every thrown-field trick species-locked. |

Two transferable thrown-field methods now sit **outside** the species-origin
count as provisional shared fieldcraft:

- `Cut the Step Twice` (`Volley`)
- `Make the Angle Bite` (`Ricochet`)

### Zarnag Novice Pass - Profile Pressure

The first Zarnag pass is a good place to increase authored species-origin
coverage for profiles that are currently rare or absent, but only where the
species truly supports them.

Profiles that Zarnag naturally help cover well:

- `Corrosion`
- `Shadow Pressure`
- `Skirmish`
- `Unpredictability`
- `Charge`
- `Rend`
- `Interruption`
- dual-wield close sequencing

Profiles Zarnag might also touch in smaller numbers:

- `Lethality`
- `Sunder`
- `Precision`

Profiles the novice pass should not force unless a concept genuinely demands
them:

- `Flow`
- `Bastion`
- additional ranged fieldcraft beyond the two provisional shared methods

### Zarnag Novice Pass - Initial Authoring Order

To keep the pass coherent, write in this order:

1. Choose the 12 attack/weapon profiles first
2. Assign each profile to its best Zarnag surface
3. Write bite and claw core first
4. Expand into daggers / short blades, then axes / cleavers
5. Add one thrown-tool Technique only where the profile truly needs it
6. Write two defenses
7. Write two resistance hybrids from secondary specializations
8. Close with the eight primary specialization Techniques

This keeps the pass profile-led, species-truthful, and resistant to surface
repetition by habit.

### Zarnag Specialization Distribution Target

The Zarnag specialization pass should show their survival realism, corpse
judgment, contamination discipline, panic management, and route-reading without
turning the species into generic “dark scouts” or “poison rogues.”

| Category | Count | Preferred specializations | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Física | 2 | `Destreza`, `Lanzamiento` | Fine dangerous handling under pressure, warning throws, route marking, and forcing distance around bad ground. |
| Mental / Aura | 3 | `Rastreo`, `Contención`, `Resonancia` | Aftermath reading, keeping contamination or panic from spilling outward, and sensing bad sites, disturbed remains, or unhealthy sympathetic pressure. |

higher-rank expansion unless a specific seed demands them.

### Zarnag Secondary Specialization Pool

These are not part of the primary 8-Technique specialization spine for the
Zarnag Novice pass. They are the short list of **secondary-fit
specializations** that still make strong sense inside the species and can be
used for:

- resistance hybrids
- deliberate swaps when a local camp or labor tradition differs
- higher-rank expansion beyond the primary Novice spine

| Specialization | Secondary fit | Best use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Trepar` | Strong | Trench walls, ruin shafts, spoilage slopes, collapsed burial lines, and hostile vertical ground where Zarnag work often continues after others stop. |
| `Supervivencia` | Strong | Broader field judgment when the Technique is about living through bad ground and route choice rather than tracking one specific disturbance history. |
| `Tolerancia` | Strong | Bodily function under disgust, infection pressure, carrion stench, long trench labor, and horror that must be endured rather than neatly solved. |
| `Medicina` | Strong | Triage, wound-read, corpse-read, infection distinction, and deciding when intervention preserves life versus spreads danger. |
| `Aplomo` | Moderate / strong by community | Visible calm under taboo, preserving social function in front of frightened outsiders, and keeping a camp or escort line from collapsing into visible panic. |
| `Agarre` | Moderate | Dragging bodies, holding someone inside quarantine, keeping a contaminated or panicked target from breaking the line, and rough contact-denial practice. |
| `Herboristería` | Moderate | Bitter preservative work, fumigants, repellents, antiseptic field blends, and low-status but essential anti-spread practice. |
| `Imitación` | Moderate by specific bands | False calls, copied distress, borrowed camp sounds, scavenger-lure behavior, or reproducing familiar signals in ways that create dangerous misreads. |

Authoring rule for Zarnag secondaries:

- If the Technique is mainly about bad terrain and reading the field, prefer
  `Rastreo` or `Supervivencia`.
- If it is mainly about withstanding environmental hostility, prefer
  `Aclimatación` or `Tolerancia`.
- If it is mainly about stopping spread, inner pressure, or outward leakage,
  prefer `Contención`.
- If it is mainly about delicate handling of dangerous material, prefer
  `Destreza` or `Medicina`.
- If it is mainly about boundary enforcement or redirected approach, prefer
  `Trampas`.
- If it is mainly about becoming hard to register, slipping through taboo
  spaces, or avoiding notice around already-panicked observers, prefer
  `Sigilo`.
- If it is mainly about sensing unhealthy resonance, disturbed remains, or bad
  sympathetic pressure in a site, prefer `Resonancia`.
- If it is mainly about copied behavior, false familiar signals, or dangerous
  mimicry, prefer `Imitación`.

What we are **not** doing here:

- turning every rot-adjacent specialization into equal Zarnag priority
- making the species only about venom and fear
- replacing corpse judgment with generic stealth, assassin, or rogue logic
