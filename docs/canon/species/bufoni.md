# Bufoni

---

## 1. Identity

**Species name:** Bufoni  
**Animal base:** Toad  
**Cultural analog:** Japanese Edo-period isolationism (sakoku, daimyo territories, shinobi tradition) + pre-Buddhist regional animism (place-specific kami, yokai as unclassified manifestations, veneration as relationship maintenance)

**First impression:** Small, still, and patient. Not obviously threatening until they act. A Bufoni in an unfamiliar space looks like someone waiting — not for anything in particular, simply waiting, as if the space has not yet earned any faster response from them. Their skin reads wrong at close range: slightly damp, textured, with visible glandular formations at the base of the skull. They smell faintly of something mineral and organic that shifts depending on where they came from. Their eyes are too large and too still.

**Deepest civilizational anxiety:** The domain cannot know whether its kunitsukami existed before the community's belief shaped it, or whether generations of sustained collective practice created what they experience as discovery.

**What they are in one sentence:** A species whose entire civilization is organized around the maintenance of sacred local relationships that may be, in part, their own creation — and that cannot survive the question.

---

## 2. Body

**Silhouette and build:** Compact and low. Shorter and broader than most humanoid species, with a wide stance and a center of gravity noticeably closer to the ground than a human analog would suggest. The posture is slightly forward-leaning at rest, as if permanently calibrated for a burst. They do not pace. They settle.

**Locomotion:** On flat ground they move efficiently but without speed emphasis — measured, deliberate, surface-reading. On uneven terrain, rock faces, or dense undergrowth they move with a confidence that looks instinctive. The hind leg musculature is disproportionately developed: not for sustained running but for explosive short-range burst. In water they are strong and quiet.

**Skin:** Slightly moist, textured with fine papillary structure. The parotoid glands — two raised formations at the base of the skull extending onto the upper back — are visible and reactive to stress. When threatened or handled, the glands secrete. The secretion is the species' most fundamental identity marker: its chemical composition is calibrated to the Bufoni's birth domain and shifts over years with prolonged environmental exposure to other territories. Under contact conditions the secretion transfers directly to another organism's skin or mucous membranes. The Bufoni themselves are resistant to their own compounds through continuous low-level exposure from birth.

**Eyes:** Large, golden-amber to dark copper, with a horizontal pupil. Very wide-angle coverage with limited focal precision — they detect movement across a broad arc better than they resolve fine detail at distance. Low-light functional. Their eyes do not track movement the way binocular predators do; they register it, all of it, in a way that can look like they are watching everything at once.

**Throat sac:** A retractable vocal sac at the base of the throat, visible when inflated. Inflated, it amplifies vocalization to a resonance range that has territorial and ritual uses. In the right acoustic conditions — a domain's specific terrain, near the kunitsukami's anchoring sites — the specific frequency pattern a Bufoni produces can be read by the local Entidad. Generations of the same family producing the same frequencies in the same territory have made this recognition reciprocal. For outsiders, the sound is simply loud.

**Natural weapon — Lengua:** The tongue is attached at the front of the jaw and extends with high speed and adhesive surface contact. Under combat stress the parotoid glands activate and the tongue carries the secretion on extension, delivering it on contact. The weapon is silent, flexible, and resolves from an angle the opponent did not track — the body stays still; the strike comes from below the chin line at 3 m range. All Bufoni have access to the Lengua from the start.

**Efecto:** Si T.A. > T.D., el objetivo realiza T.R. Veneno. Si falla, queda afectado por el compuesto. Severidad escala por rango del portador: 1–2 Leve, 3–4 Moderado, 5–6 Grave. El efecto específico del compuesto varía por linaje — ver variantes de clan (pendiente de sesión de diseño).

**Senses:** Visual field is the primary sense for detection. Vibration sensitivity through ground contact is moderate — useful for detecting movement in familiar terrain, limited in unfamiliar environments. No equivalent to Arakhel vibratory network reading. Olfactory reading of toxin composition is sharp: a Bufoni can identify another Bufoni's domain origin by close proximity or direct contact, and detect when a signature is inconsistent or has been modified.

**Physical baseline:**
- Height: 1.35–1.60 m
- Weight: 60–90 kg
- Biological lifespan: 55–75 years
- Actual lifespan: considerably lower; domain defenders rarely reach biological maximum

## 3. Culture

**Fundamental unit:** The domain. Not the family, not the city, not the clan in the lineage sense — the domain as a territory with a living kunitsukami relationship. Everything else is organized around the domain's continuity.

**Internal hierarchy:**
- **The daimyo-equivalent** — domain head, holds political authority and performs the territorial vocal claim that outside parties recognize. Their legitimacy depends entirely on whether the kunitsukami recognizes their voice in succession. Without that recognition, authority is contested regardless of lineage.
- **The ritual guardian class** — the family or families who hold the accumulated knowledge of the domain's kunitsukami relationship: the correct ceremonial sequence, the seasonal ritual timings, the specific offerings the Entidad has responded to across generations, the vocal frequency that the succession ritual requires. This knowledge is not written. It lives in people. The daimyo cannot function without them; the ritual guardians cannot project political authority without the daimyo. The tension between these two is the most stable internal fault line in every domain.
- **The domain population** — the rest of the domain. Hunters, fabricators, domain maintainers, those who produce and sustain the material conditions the domain requires.
- **Shinobi** — outside the hierarchy, operating in other domains' territory. They exist by necessity but occupy an ambiguous social position. Necessary. Somewhat contaminated by function. The domain does not ask what they saw. The shinobi does not say.

**Prestige:** Depth of kunitsukami relationship, domain longevity, ritual accuracy, and correct guidance. The most respected Bufoni in a domain is not the strongest or the wealthiest — it is the one who has been reading the domain's spirit the longest and has never given wrong guidance based on that reading. Giving incorrect guidance when you knew the hilo said something else is the deepest disgrace.

**Admired:** Fidelity, patience, accumulated knowledge of the specific. A Bufoni who has spent thirty years in the same territory and can describe how the kunitsukami responded to drought differently in three distinct decades is worth more than one who has survived more dramatic encounters and can prove it.

**Disgraced:** Revealing domain knowledge to outsiders. Misreading the kunitsukami and not saying so. Giving guidance that led someone to harm because you confused what you wanted to be true with what the domain was actually saying.

**Relationship to ruins:** Domain-specific. Domains near pre-catastrophe ruins have incorporated them into their theology as sites of particular amatsukami pressure — places where the boundary between the physical world and the cosmological layer is unstable. Some domains treat adjacent ruins as sacred warning zones. Others have built their ritual geography around specific ruins as anchor points. What they share is that no domain enters a ruin casually, and the domain that has read its adjacent ruins across generations holds something no outside researcher can access.

## 4. Theology and Cosmology

**Core animist premise:** Every place has spirit. The relationship between a community and the spirit of its place requires generational presence, practice, and offering. The spirit is not requested or commanded — it is recognized, maintained, and read. No two places have the same spirit. No two domains have the same theology.

**The Tauma mapping:**

| Animist term | Bufoni understanding | Actual Tauma category |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yaoyorozu | "Spirits in everything" — the presence felt in old stone, deep water, ancient trees, bones of the earth | Vestigios — Tauma imprints accumulated in matter across geological time in high-gradient zones |
| Ujigami | Clan-protective spirit, maintained through ancestral objects and shrine sites, specific to a lineage | Vínculo — active Limbo entity connection through a specific object; the entity uses the family as a crossing point |
| Kunitsukami | Local land spirit, specific to the domain's geography, shaped by centuries of relationship | Entidad or Soberano — regional Tauma entity shaped by environmental conditions and sustained collective belief |
| Amatsukami | Primordial heavenly spirits, cosmological scale, associated with the structure of the world itself | Abismales — pre-cataclysm ecosystemic entities that ARE their zones; acknowledged cosmologically, not interacted with directly |

**The Noragami logic:** The Bufoni believe that veneration sustains the kunitsukami — that a spirit neglected long enough weakens and eventually either dies or turns malignant. This belief is functional: it motivates the ritual maintenance that genuinely does shape how the Entidad expresses itself. It is also structurally wrong: when a domain falls, the kunitsukami does not weaken or turn against the world. It continues, expressing its underlying nature without the centuries of relational shaping that made it legible. What the Bufoni call a "corrupted kami" is an active Entidad that no living being has the ritual knowledge to navigate. The domain cannot know this from inside; knowing it from outside cannot be communicated back in without damage.

**Sacred history:** Domain-specific. There is no universal Bufoni creation myth. Each domain has its own account of what the catastrophe was — generally interpreted through the lens of the amatsukami, as a cosmological disruption caused by forces above the kunitsukami level. Some domains believe the amatsukami retreated. Others believe they collapsed into the Tauma gradient. Others do not speculate, only noting that the old connections are harder now. What nearly all domains share is the conviction that their kunitsukami survived the catastrophe because someone was there to maintain the relationship through it. The oldest domains claim they have never stopped.

**Yokai:** Unclassified local Tauma manifestations that fall outside normal ritual relationship. Not all Entidades in Bufoni territory are kunitsukami with which the domain has working relationships. Some are environmental, old, and behave according to their own logic. Domains that have read their yokai across enough generations can sometimes predict their behavior without understanding its source. Others simply mark those territories and do not enter.

**What they get wrong:** The belief that abandoned kunitsukami die or corrupt. The belief that their domain's theology is true in the revelatory sense rather than the relational one. The specific belief in the ritual guardian class that the knowledge they hold is complete — that there are no aspects of the kunitsukami's nature that their vocabulary has not yet named.

## 5. Organization and Internal Variation

**Three categories of Bufoni in the world:**

**Domain Bufoni:** The majority. Living in their domain, contributing to its continuity, calibrated to its kunitsukami. Their entire world is their domain in a biological sense: their toxin, their vocal register, their sensory baseline — all formed around that specific place. The world outside the domain is foreign in a way that other species do not experience the same way.

**Neutral-site custodians:** Families that have managed inter-domain aggregation points across enough generations that their biological signature belongs to no single domain. Their toxin composition marks them as belonging to the neutral site. Their ujigami is the kunitsukami of that site — a Soberano- or Abismal-scale entity that no single domain claims. They have no domain allegiance. They are recognized by all, trusted by none in the way a domain member trusts their own. They are the only Bufoni that most outside species ever meet with any regularity. Some neutral-site families have been in this role for so long they have genuinely lost access to the kind of deep domain-specific reading that makes domain Bufoni what they are; what they have instead is breadth — knowledge of dozens of domains' surface behavior without deep access to any of them.

**Domain survivors:** Bufoni whose domain no longer exists. Their biological calibration points to a place whose kunitsukami is no longer being maintained in the form they knew. The entity is still there — they can feel it, distantly, through the site-fidelity pull their body continues to generate — but the relationship that made it navigable has ended. They cannot rebuild it alone; rebuilding a kunitsukami relationship requires a community, generational time, and territory. Most domain survivors do not attempt this. Some attach to other domains as subordinate presences (unusual and socially complicated; their toxin marks them as foreign). Some reach neutral sites and remain in the margin. Some enter the wider world entirely. Formix cities are among the few places where their context-free status is not immediately a social liability — the free Formix have no generational framework to evaluate what was lost.

**Internal tensions:**

*Daimyo vs. ritual guardians:* The domain cannot function without either, and each holds something the other cannot replace. This tension is usually managed by convention and accumulated precedent. It becomes dangerous when succession creates a daimyo whose voice the kunitsukami has not learned, or when the ritual guardian family becomes too small to hold all the knowledge safely.

*Old domains vs. new domains:* Domains that survived the catastrophe with an unbroken ritual chain have kunitsukami relationships of enormous depth and specificity. Domains established afterward have genuine relationships — the kunitsukami is real — but the accumulated vocabulary, the seasonal knowledge, the specific understanding of how that entity responds to crisis, has not yet had time to develop. Whether what the oldest domains have can be rebuilt by a new domain, or whether something in the pre-catastrophe accumulation cannot be recovered, is the sharpest unresolved disagreement in Bufoni inter-domain discourse.

*The shinobi question:* Domains do not discuss this openly, but the shinobi's biological remapping is known to be permanent. A shinobi who successfully completes years of operational service in another domain returns with the wrong toxin, a modified vocal register, and whatever their presence in multiple kunitsukami territories has done to their relationship with their own ujigami. The domain needs them. The domain also knows what it is asking of them.

## 6. Daily Life and Life Cycle

**Subsistence:** Primarily hunting, using toad-biology ambush techniques carried into intelligent practice — patience, positioning, precise timing. The domain territory is understood in sufficient detail that a skilled hunter knows which approach routes animals use, what the terrain does in each season, and where the kunitsukami's activity concentrates prey. Fishing in domains near water sources. Supplemented by what viajero offerings bring at aggregation points, and by inter-domain material exchange during periodic gatherings.

**Childhood:** Learning the domain's kunitsukami before learning anything else. Not in the abstract — the specific domain. This cruce. The specific way the Entidad responds to the seasonal ritual in the third month. The specific vocalization the domain's elder makes at dawn to maintain the territorial claim. The specific spots in the domain where the yaoyorozu density is high and children do not run. The specific tone of the ujigami's response when the family shrine is approached correctly versus when something is wrong. This knowledge does not transfer. A child from another domain would find it meaningless. A child from this domain absorbs it the way their skin absorbs the local chemistry — not as information but as baseline.

**Shinobi selection:** Happens before the biological signature fully fixes. The domain identifies candidates young, before their parotoid composition and vocal register have hardened into their permanent form. The selection is not announced widely. The families involved understand what they are agreeing to. The selected child is not immediately removed — the remapping is gradual, managed through shifts in diet, water source, and eventually territory, over years. By the time the shinobi is operationally ready, they have a different biological identity than the one they were born with.

**Rite of passage (non-shinobi):** Formal recognition by the domain's kunitsukami at maturity — a specific ceremony in which the new adult presents themselves to the kunitsukami anchor site for the first time as an individual rather than as a child of the family. The entity does not speak or appear. What happens is that the domain's ritual guardian class, who can read the kunitsukami's response through the anchor objects, observes whether the recognition is mutual. A young adult whose recognition is uncertain is not disgraced — but something is noted, and watched.

**Death and mourning:** The specific reading a Bufoni has developed of their domain's kunitsukami over a lifetime is individual and non-transferable. When they die, the domain loses that specific interpretation. The ritual guardian class can document what the deceased knew, but documentation of kunitsukami knowledge is structurally incomplete — the most important knowledge is the embodied kind that comes from decades of presence and cannot be rendered in words without losing something. Domains that lose experienced members lose depth that takes generations to rebuild. This makes death in inter-domain conflict specifically costly: not only the person, but the reading.

**Elderhood:** The ideal is to become so specific to the domain that you are almost indistinguishable from its spirit without losing the clarity that you are distinct. A respected elder can say what the kunitsukami's current behavior would have meant thirty years ago, before a specific event changed how the domain relates to the entity. They can read the difference between normal variation and genuine change in the entity's expression. The risk of deep elderhood is losing the ability to function outside the domain without significant disorientation — and the domain sometimes does not distinguish between that loss and the deepening of sacred relationship.

## 7. External Relations

**Geographic spread:** Wherever toad biology is viable — wetlands, forest edges, transitional terrain between environments, the margins of bodies of water. Domains prefer terrain that provides natural shelter and defensibility without requiring extensive construction. The most ancient domains are in ecologically stable zones that have not changed dramatically since pre-catastrophe. Newer domains occupy what was available after the catastrophe's reorganization.

**How other species encounter them:** Most Bufoni never leave their domain and are never encountered. The Bufoni of a domain know when someone approaches their territory. Non-Bufoni who enter without following the offering protocol are navigating a space whose rules they do not know, managed by people who already know everything that territory can tell them about an intruder. The experience of not encountering the Bufoni of a domain is not neutral; it is the domain operating exactly as intended.

The Bufoni that other species actually meet are neutral-site custodians (at aggregation points, often the only visible face of the species) and domain survivors (in the wider world, usually without context, carrying a biological calibration that belongs to a place that no longer maintains its end of the relationship).

**Relationships:**

*To the Rokhart:* They want observation posts and cannot have them under conditions their methods allow. A Rokhart wants to study the relationship between collective practice and domain presence without interfering, but the offering protocol requires identity and history. For a Rokhart, recording without interfering is ideal. For a Bufoni, entering the domain without a relationship is already interference.

*To the Naghii:* Both organize their relationship with territory and the extranatural around presences that respond to accumulated collective practice. Tension arises because each species can partially read what the other does. A Naghii might see in a domain presence an ancient, poorly translated signal structure. A Bufoni might see in Naghii archives a way to touch presence without feeding a relationship.

*To the Drak'kai:* They can read a signature in an active domain that their own knowledge immediately recognizes. What they read does not always match what the domain describes. A Drak'kai might point out that a domain's accumulation resembles places where the relationship eventually devoured those who sustained it. A Bufoni guardian might respond that a relationship without risk is not a relationship.

*To the Arakhel:* They have nodes in the between that surround domains. Some Bufoni families have working relationships with certain nodes—a specific transit protocol that benefits both. The Arakhel knows who crossed the between before touching the domain; the Bufoni knows what happens when the traveler enters an active territorial relationship. Otherwise, misunderstanding is the norm: an Arakhel asks if the crossing accepted being traversed, while a Bufoni asks if the domain recognized the presence.

*To the Zarnag:* They work sites where something died without closing. Collapsed Bufoni domains are exactly this kind of site. A Zarnag might say the site is infected with Residue. A Bufoni might say the domain presence continues speaking without guardians. Both phrases can describe the same horror.

*To the Ceratox:* They react to what an active domain accumulates as if the entire site were under high pressure. This response activates their biology in ways Bufoni find difficult to manage: an alerted Ceratox can destabilize relationships that took generations to establish. Interactions tend to be brief and arranged outside the main territory.

*To the Formix:* They are the most open species to survivors of fallen domains. They have no history of inter-domain relations, no expectations of what a territory or domain presence should be. A Bufoni from a fallen domain can find in a Formix community the only place where no one expects their territory's protocol to remain functional. This can be a relief, or feel like a second death.

*To the Loxod:* They can receive the chemical and vibratory load of an ancient domain with a depth that makes guardians uncomfortable. Where the Bufoni reads relationship, the Loxod might receive accumulation, grief, fear, offering, and loss. A Loxod might name burdens that the Bufoni must continue treating as sacred presence so as not to break.

*To the Ursari:* They understand the relationship between body, site, and practice. A forge and a domain teach that the place participates in what is done there. An Ursari might respect Bufoni discipline but distrust a culture where the soil decides authority. A Bufoni might see in the Ursari forge a powerful form of incomplete relationship: much attention to the material, less to the presence the place accumulated.

*To the Luphran:* They are difficult inside a domain. Their nervous activation, crew logic, and tendency to read authority in terms of functional command do not fit Bufoni protocol. A Luphran crew might respect a passage agreement, but if the situation changes quickly, the captain will act for crew survival rather than sensitivity to the domain presence. The Bufoni do not forget these incidents. The domain doesn't always forget them either.

*To the Panin:* They are unpredictable and therefore dangerous. They touch, taste, jump, interrupt, follow immediate signals, and can trigger territorial responses before explanations are complete. Yet, a Panin might detect a fresh alteration in the domain faster than a guardian accustomed to justifying variations as tradition.

*To the Chelicer:* They see in Bufoni protocols something close yet alien. Both believe that correct conditions allow access to a truth that shouldn't be improvised. But the Chelicer seeks a verdict under a controlled procedure; the Bufoni sustains a relationship in living territory. To a Chelicer, the domain's variability can seem like impurity. To a Bufoni, a truth that doesn't change when touching the soil might never have been alive.

*To the Sauri:* They might see a Bufoni domain as a poorly defined vessel: a territorial relationship that filters a presence without sufficient visible architecture. To the Bufoni, this criticism confuses life with disorder. Tension appears when a domain starts responding in new ways. The Sauri asks how to contain it. The Bufoni asks what protocol they forgot to listen to.

*To the Myo:* They unsettle guardians and domain lords alike. A Myo might detect changes in presence before the protocol admits them, stating that a domain's response changed direction or that the water dreams of a shape not in the records. Some domains consult them in secret. Others expel them before they can turn ritual doubt into a political crisis.

## 8. Material Expression and Language

**Architecture:** Low, terrain-integrated, not announced. A domain's built structures are meant to read as part of the landscape to anyone approaching without prior knowledge of their location. The most important structures are not the largest — they are the ritual anchor sites, which may be entirely unmarked from the outside. The domain's defensive geometry is the terrain itself, managed and understood rather than built.

**Ritual objects:** The most critical material culture is the ujigami anchor — the specific object through which the family's vínculo is maintained. These objects are not always visually impressive. A smooth river stone carried for nine generations, a piece of pre-catastrophe material recovered from a ruin near the domain's founding site, a dried compound of local biological material assembled during a critical moment in the domain's history. What makes the object significant is the accumulated relationship, not its appearance. Destroying an ujigami anchor object is the most targeted act of warfare available in the Bufoni world.

**Toxin-derived materials:** Domain-specific pigments extracted from local biology (plants, fungi, small fauna that feed in the domain's territory) produce colors and compounds that mark objects as being of that domain. Cloth and ritual items dyed with these compounds carry the domain's material signature. A Bufoni can identify the domain origin of an object by chemical reading in the same way they read a person's toxin signature.

**Writing and recording:** Domains document what is safe to document — material inventory, genealogy, geographic records, seasonal timing charts. The most critical ritual knowledge is intentionally not written. This is not because they lack writing — it is because the guardian class understands that the knowledge is partially constituted by the embodied relationship through which it is held. A written version would be incomplete in a way that is more dangerous than no version: it would create a false sense of completeness while missing the aspects that only live in practice. What is written about the kunitsukami is the historical record (what happened, when, what offering was made, what response was observed). What is not written is why the response meant what it meant.

**Language:** A base language shared across all Bufoni communities, recognizable as unified in structure. Heavy dialectal variation per domain — phonology, vocabulary, idiom — to the degree that two domain Bufoni from geographically distant areas may find each other somewhat difficult to follow before adjustment. The ritual and sacred vocabulary is the most domain-specific layer: terms for aspects of the kunitsukami that other domains' languages have not needed to name, specific words for specific phenomena in specific terrain. Shinobi must master the target domain's dialect and sacred vocabulary as part of remapping. Their accent carries longer than their toxin — a shinobi may pass a chemical reading and still be identified by pronunciation.

**Naming:** Domain-specific naming conventions. The name marks origin in a way that the toxin confirms. A domain survivor who introduces themselves using their origin domain's naming convention is identifying themselves to anyone who knows that domain. Some survivors change their naming pattern. Some do not.

## 9. System Connections

**Characteristics:** +1 **Agilidad**, **Sabiduría**, **Astucia**
- *Agilidad:* burst movement, patient positioning, shinobi agility, surface reading in difficult terrain
- *Sabiduría:* ritual depth, kunitsukami reading, generational knowledge of the specific, recognition of what the domain is actually saying vs. what one wants it to say
- *Astucia:* intelligence gathering, pattern recognition, toxin-signature reading, shinobi strategic thinking

**Herencia — Piel Permeable:** The same cutaneous permeability that enables parotoid toxin production and environmental chemical reading also absorbs hostile compounds faster than other species. Bufoni receive a permanent −3 penalty to T.R. (Veneno). Not a cultural sensitivity or learned condition — it is the physics of a skin with no absorption barrier. The same surface that makes the Lengua work is the same surface that makes them vulnerable to it.

**Competency affinities:**
- Sigilo — ambush biology, domain knowledge
- Supervivencia — terrain reading in known territory
- Rastreo — toxin-signature tracking of other Bufoni through territory
- Intuición — kunitsukami reading, ritual awareness
- Sabiduría-based ritual specialization rolls

**Natural weapon — Lengua:** T.A. Agilidad / T.I. Agilidad / Alcance 3 m / d6 / Torsión · Interrupción · Impredecibilidad · Corrosión. Toxin triggers on any hit (T.A. > T.D.): target makes T.R. Veneno, severity scales by rango (1–2 Minor, 3–4 Moderate, 5–6 Severe). Specific compound effect varies by lineage — pending clan variation design session.

**Armor identity:** Light to medium. Heavy armor limits skin contact with the environment and impedes the sensory biology that reads terrain and toxin signatures. Some domains have developed medium armor traditions for specific roles; shinobi wear nothing that interferes with biological signature management.

**Resistance:** Partial resistance to Veneno-category effects — biological, from constant low-level parotoid self-exposure. Severity: partial, not immunity. Exact threshold to be determined in system balance.

**Technique implications:**
- Zone control through patience and positioning (ambush setup, reading approach vectors)
- Toxin application as Legado expansion of the natural weapon
- Ritual/kunitsukami interaction (domain-specific, for domain Bufoni with active relationship)
- Information-gathering and signature-reading (Astucia/Sabiduría-based)
- Shinobi traditions likely as specialization-level techniques rather than innate species

**Confirmed Legado design:**

**Legado 1 — Sabiduría Corporal (skill bonus):** Sabiduría replaces the default characteristic for T.E. de Saltar, Nadar, and Sigilo. +1 per 4 NR on all three. The Bufoni's jump is body knowledge of that exact surface; their swim is rhythmic pattern, not brute force; their stealth is knowing when not to move, not how to move fast.

**Legado 2 — passive property on Lengua (T.I. bonus while hidden):** While the Bufoni is Oculto and T.A. > T.D., the attack adds bonus impact dice by rango:
- Rango 1–2: +1d6
- Rango 3–4: +2d6
- Rango 5–6: +3d6
The predator that waited longest hits hardest when it moves.

**Legado 3 — Golpe Sin Eco (Reactivo - Utilidad · Ritmo 0 · Desgaste 2):** Trigger: after a Lengua attack from Oculto, in the moment the state would be compromised. At non-melee range: no enemy attempts Percepción; the attack generates no locatable signal; Oculto remains intact. At melee range: the technique includes an immediate repositioning movement before the source can be identified; if that movement reaches a valid Oculto-sustaining position, the effect applies; if no valid position exists, the technique produces no effect. Restricted to Lengua attacks.

## 10. Player Character Hooks and Open Questions

**What a Bufoni PC knows:**
- The specific terrain and kunitsukami of their origin domain in detail that no outside source could provide
- How to read toxin signatures of other Bufoni (domain identification)
- The offering protocol and what it does and does not actually accomplish
- That collapsed domain territories are dangerous and that the danger is not what other species think it is

**What they likely do not know:**
- What is actually happening in collapsed domain territories — the Entidad vs. corrupted-kami distinction is not accessible from inside the Bufoni cosmology
- What the kunitsukami's nature is independent of the relationship — no Bufoni does
- Whether their domain's ujigami anchor is still active if they have been away long enough
- What the Drak'kai know about their domain's territory if a Drak'kai has ever passed through it

**Why one might leave:**

*Domain survivor:* The domain fell — destroyed by inter-domain shinobi warfare, catastrophic kunitsukami destabilization, demographic collapse, or the specific horror of a kunitsukami whose nature drifted so far from its relational form that the domain could no longer navigate it. The survivor's biology still pulls toward the site. The site no longer has the relationship. What they carry is accumulated domain knowledge that has nowhere to be applied.

*Active shinobi on long assignment:* Operational, possibly without active contact with their origin domain. Their mission may be complete. Their domain may not have survived to receive them. They are the domain's most capable external agent and the one the domain cannot fully reclaim.

*Ritual guardian seeking outside knowledge:* Something changed in their domain's kunitsukami — a response that doesn't fit the accumulated record, a behavior the ritual vocabulary has no name for. They are seeking context: Drak'kai cosmological reading of what they observed, Zarnag site-reading of nearby collapsed domains, Arakhel route knowledge that might explain what has been moving through the between near their territory.

*Young domain-member before full calibration:* Sent out by the domain for a specific purpose before their biological signature is fully fixed. This is rare but creates a character who is still in the process of becoming what they will be.

*Neutral-site custodian who left the neutral site:* Has no domain allegiance, belongs nowhere in the Bufoni internal geography, and has chosen to move into the wider world rather than remain at the threshold between domains. Knows more about inter-domain surface behavior than any domain Bufoni; knows less about any specific domain's depth than any domain member.

**What a player character from this species fears losing first:** The domain. Not abstractly — the specific place whose kunitsukami is the only Tauma entity in the world whose ritual vocabulary they fully hold. When that is gone, what remains is someone who knows a great deal about a relationship that no longer exists in its prior form.
