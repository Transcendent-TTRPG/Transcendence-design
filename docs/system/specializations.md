# Specializations

Specializations are specific skills a character can develop. They reflect both theoretical knowledge and practical mastery.

Each specialization is associated with a characteristic. Reaching a new rank grants +1 to that characteristic (Synapsis).

## Categories

| Category                  | Core aptitudes        | Development method |
|---|---|---|
| Physical Skills           | STR, AGI, TEN         | Training, intensive practice |
| Mental Skills             | INT, WIS, CUN         | Study, observation, intellectual practice |
| Social and Emotional      | CMP, AUR, PRE         | Communication, social dynamics, interpersonal management |
| Arts and Crafts           | WIS                   | Formal learning, practice, creative experimentation |
| Lore                      | INT                   | Detailed study, investigation, practical application |

### Physical Skills
Require bodily dexterity, strength, agility, and control.
Essential for survival in hostile environments, acrobatic maneuvers, and combat performance.
Examples: Jump (Horizontal, Vertical, Precision, Obstacle, Power)

### Mental Skills
Include intellect, wisdom, and cunning.
Based on critical thinking, analysis, and application of theory in practical situations.

### Social and Emotional Skills
Focus on effective interaction and communication.
Essential for leading, persuading, deceiving, and negotiating.

### Arts and Crafts
Combine theory and practical skill.
- **Crafts**: technical/manual abilities (smithing, carpentry, alchemy)
- **Arts**: creative expression (music, painting, dance)

### Lore
Deep specialized knowledge across disciplines (alchemy, medicine, astronomy).
Requires detailed study and practical application.

## Starting Specializations

Every character begins with **4 starting specializations** at Level 1 / Rank 1:

- **3 from background** — chosen according to background category restrictions
- **1 universal: Vigor** — granted automatically to every character regardless of background

The same specialization cannot be chosen twice at creation.
All other specializations begin at Level 0 (Untrained).

### Vigor — Universal Starting Specialization

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Associated characteristic | Tenacity |
| Starting level | 1 |
| Starting rank | Novice |
| Synapsis at creation | +1 Tenacity |

Vigor represents the minimum physical endurance, effort adaptation, and tolerance to adverse conditions required to survive in this world. Every playable creature has developed at least an elemental degree of vigor before becoming an adventurer.

- Does **not** replace species bonuses to Tenacity — both accumulate.
- Unless a specific rule states otherwise, every character gains at least +1 to Tenacity from Vigor.

## Specialization Roll

`S.R. = 1d10 + Specialization Level + Competency Rank + Associated Characteristic + Bonuses`
