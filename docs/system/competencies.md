# Competencies

Competencies represent degree of mastery in a specific field. They modify rolls and allow progression through use.

Characteristics express potential. Competencies express how much that potential has been trained and refined.

## Ranks

A new rank is gained every 3 competency levels.

| Rank      | Level Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Untrained | 0           | Starting level for most competencies |
| Novice    | 1–3         | Granted by background specializations at creation |
| Adept     | 4–6         | |
| Expert    | 7–9         | |
| Master    | 10+         | |

## Progression Requirements

| Specialization type        | Progress points to level up |
|---|---|
| Major affinity (background)| 5 points |
| All other competencies     | 10 points |

Progression only occurs through Learning Advantage and only when the challenge is appropriate to current rank.

### Appropriate test difficulty by rank

| Rank      | Minimum test difficulty |
|---|---|
| Novice    | Any |
| Adept     | Fundamental or higher |
| Expert    | Challenging or higher |
| Master    | Rigorous or higher |

## Competency Types

### Weapons
Mastery with a specific weapon type.

- Each **level**: +1 to A.R.
- Each **rank**: +1 to A.R. + one additional die to I.R.
- Progression: hit and deal damage with the weapon in relevant encounters.

### Armors
Efficient use of armor.

- Each **level**: block +1
- Reaching **Expert**: movement speed penalty reduced by one category
- Reaching **Master**: penalty reduced by one additional category
- Progression: receive a hit where armor effectively reduces damage.

### Shields
Technical mastery of shield use.

- Each **rank**: access to additional shield maneuvers (type-dependent)
- Progression: successfully use martial shield maneuvers.

### Evasion
Anticipating and avoiding attacks.

- Each **level**: +1 to D.R.
- Progression: successfully avoid relevant attacks through mobility and reflexes.

### Specialization
Technical mastery in a specific skill.

- Each **level**: +1 to S.R.
- Each **rank**: +1 to S.R.
- Progression: overcome tests appropriate to current rank.

### Resistances
Withstanding specific types of danger. Often develops through exposure and survival.

| Resistance type   | Bonus per level |
|---|---|
| Elemental damage  | +1 to R.R. against elemental effects |
| Poison            | +1 to R.R. against poisons |
| Infection         | +1 to R.R. against infections |
| Affliction        | +1 to R.R. against afflictions; +1 to Affliction R.R. per rank during cognitive meditation |
| Alteration        | +1 to R.R. against alterations |
| Curses            | +1 to detect or resist curses |
