# Limbo Manifestations

**Authority:** `data/system/limbo-manifestations.yaml`

The Limbo is a parallel dimension imperceptible to normal creatures and the origin of Tauma energy. It does not interact with the physical world directly — its energy filters through in three distinct forms. These manifestations are the substrate behind extranatural conditions and Tauma use.

Traditions that have systematized knowledge of the Limbo interpret its phenomena through their own doctrinal frameworks. These are theological interpretations, not the mechanical cause.

---

## Three manifestation types

### Ambient flow (Flujo ambiental)

Continuous passive filtration of Limbo energy into a physical area. Not anchored to any object or entity — diffuse, environmental. Can be constant (naturally permeable zones) or temporary (residual after an intense event).

- Passive: no active agent required
- Diffuse: permeates the area, not a single point
- Variable: intensity fluctuates based on proximity to links and past events
- Not automatically harmful: only generates extranatural conditions when intense enough

An intense ambient flow can raise the severity of extranatural conditions in the area. See [environmental-conditions.md](environmental-conditions.md).

---

### Vestige (Vestigio)

Residual Tauma energy deposited in a physical object by past Limbo contact. No
entity or link is currently active — it is only the imprint left behind. Fades
gradually; not permanent.

- Residual: the source is no longer present
- Fading: weakens over time
- Localized: in a specific object, not diffuse
- Inert: does not generate active effects on its own

**Key distinction:** a vestige is not a link. There is no active connection to the Limbo. It is the impression the energy left, not the energy itself — like residual heat in a stone after the fire is gone.

### Vestige principles

- Limited physical object: a vestige is carried by a real object
- No energy creation: it biases, synchronizes, or filters an existing physical
  process rather than producing a phenomenon from nothing
- Local causality: it always requires a medium such as light, sound,
  vibration, pressure, heat, or another real carrier
- Pattern fatigue: its usable pattern depletes unpredictably; the exact
  remaining uses should stay hidden from players
- Moderate power: it creates leverage, not hierarchy-defining supremacy
- Cumulative Affliction pressure: each meaningful use increases the user's
  Affliction intensity and may eventually manifest or worsen a sensory
  Affliction
- Weak option: among Limbo-bearing objects, vestiges are the lesser but safer
  option because they have bounded effects and hidden finite uses

### Discovery rule

A vestige is not normally usable on first contact.

- Undiscovered state: the bearer only knows a mysterious presentation and
  cannot intentionally activate the object
- Discovered state: the bearer has understood enough of the vestige's true
  pattern to use it intentionally

Once discovered, the bearer should gain access at minimum to:

- the real name
- its linked sense
- its usage requirements
- its bounded effect identity

Transferring a vestige to another bearer should normally reset discovery unless
the fiction explicitly says that the new bearer inherits the understanding.

### Authoring note

Vestiges should usually be built from real-world mythic, historical,
superstitious, or folkloric object patterns, then translated into
Transcendence's mechanical logic rather than invented as arbitrary fantasy
artifacts.

---

### Link (Vínculo)

An active bridge between the physical world and the Limbo carried by a physical
object. Energy flows continuously through it while the user sustains a real
connection to the linked entity. More powerful and more dangerous than a
vestige.

- Active: the connection to the Limbo is continuous
- Anchored: to a specific object
- Generates ambient flow in its surrounding area as a secondary effect
- Origin: natural (zones where dimensions are thin) or created through Tauma practice (open design — D-11)

An active link generates ambient flow in its surroundings. A sufficiently powerful link can produce persistent extranatural conditions in the area.

### Link principles

- Object with active relation: a link is still a physical object, but unlike a
  vestige it maintains an ongoing connection to an entity or force of the
  Limbo
- More powerful option: links are the stronger class of Limbo-bearing objects
- No finite hidden uses: the limit is not stored charges the way a vestige
  depletes
- Scales through Affliction: stronger access depends on how deep the linked
  sensory Affliction has progressed
- Cost is Cordura pressure: using the link draws on the relationship and should
  eventually be paid through sanity/Cordura consequences rather than object
  depletion

### Link use requirements

The bearer must have at least one active **Affliction** on the sense required by
the link.

- Single-sense link: use the severity of that sense's Affliction
- Multi-sense link: use the **lowest** severity among all required senses

If the bearer does not have the required linked Affliction, the link cannot be
used at all.

This creates the intended asymmetry:

- vestiges are the weaker object class: finite, partially mysterious, and
  pressure-building through repeated use
- links are the stronger object class: they create a true bond to a Limbo
  entity and trade power for worsening dependence and eventual Cordura cost

### Authoring note — links

Like vestiges, links should usually begin from real-world mythic, historical,
superstitious, or folkloric object patterns and then be translated into the
setting's Limbo logic.

## Relationships between manifestations

| Relationship | Direction | Mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flow → Vestige | Possible | Intense sustained flow can deposit vestiges in nearby objects even after the flow stops |
| Link → Flow | Always | Every active link generates ambient flow in its surrounding area |
| Vestige ≠ Link | Distinction | A vestige has no active Limbo connection; matters for detection and environmental effects |

## Detection

Normal creatures cannot perceive the Limbo directly. Detection requires:

| Capability | What it detects |
| --- | --- |
| **Instinct** | Primal non-rational response — senses something does not belong; imprecise |
| **Resonance** | Active directed sounding of an object or area — confirms Tauma is present, distinguishes active (link) from residual (vestige), estimates relative intensity; more precise than Instinct but no structural detail |
| **Thaumaturgy** | Identifies taumatic structure and dimensional origin; distinguishes flow vs. vestige vs. link by active vs. fading signature |
| **Theology** | Recognizes markers of Limbo presence through the doctrine of any tradition that has systematized this knowledge |

Detection and discovery are different things.

- detection answers whether something Limbo-linked is present
- discovery answers whether a specific vestige can be intentionally used
- link use answers whether the bearer's Affliction state is sufficient to
  sustain the connection
