# General Rules

## Dice

| Die  | Shape        | Primary use |
|---|---|---|
| d4   | Tetrahedral  | Low-range outcomes, minor damage |
| d6   | Cubic        | Moderate outcomes |
| d8   | Octahedral   | Intermediate outcomes and damage |
| d10  | Decahedral   | Most game checks (primary die) |
| d12  | Dodecahedral | High outcomes and damage |
| d20  | Icosahedral  | Checks related to creature health |

## Core Rules

### Specific-over-General
If a specific rule conflicts with a general rule, the specific rule takes precedence.

### Narrative Flexibility
The Narrator may adapt, modify, or omit rules to strengthen narrative flow.
Adjustments must be: **fair, consistent, transparent**.

### Rounding
Always round **up** to the nearest whole number, unless a specific rule states otherwise.
Applies to: damage, durations, ratings, and other numeric values.

### Strongest Condition
When a creature receives multiple bonuses or penalties of the same type, only the most significant applies.
If a new effect is stronger (or equally strong but longer), it replaces and resets the existing one.

### Tool Handling
Crafting, repairing, refining, and specialization activities require task-specific tools.
If exact tools are unavailable, similar tools may be used at higher difficulty (Narrator evaluates viability).

## Adventure Cycle

### Exploration Phase
Players explore locations, uncover mysteries, seek resources, interact with the world.
Activities: environment assessment, maintenance, rest and recovery, planning.

### Conflict Phase
Resolves high-risk encounters, combat, and tactical obstacles.

**Key terms:**
- **Creature**: any entity in play (PC, NPC, or Monster)
- **PC (Player Character)**: protagonist controlled by a player
- **NPC (Non-Player Character)**: world figure controlled by the Narrator
- **Monster**: NPC subtype focused on instinctive or unnatural behavior

**Conflict flow:**
1. Narrator sets conflict conditions
2. Place creature tokens on initiative track
3. Leftmost creature acts
4. Active creature reviews status, picks intent, chooses action
5. Creature declares intent
6. Possible reactions are checked
7. Interruptions or responses resolved
8. Main action resolved
9. Move token on initiative track
10. After all creatures activate, repeat from step 3 until conflict ends

### Interlude Phase
Extended period for activities outside detailed scene-by-scene play.
Activities: gear creation/improvement, recovery, resource management, planning, personal development.

## NPCs and Creatures

NPCs and monsters do not follow the same progression rules as player characters.
They have **Traits** — advantages, disadvantages, or special capabilities tied to nature, behavior, environment, emotional state, or combat role.
