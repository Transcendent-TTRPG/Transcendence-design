## Approved rule

- Reactive utility, Novice or higher Alchemy; cost **Rhythm 3 / Attrition 1**.
- Before defense against an attack from a poisoned weapon, select one exposed
  dose of a poison type you know. Its point on the weapon must be physically
  accessible to the neutralizer and within **1 meter of the practitioner**.
  This is not the reach of the practitioner's weapon.
- Have one compatible neutralizer dose already prepared and ready to apply,
  of equal or higher quality than that preparation. No instant synthesis,
  drawing from inaccessible storage, free identification or inferred quality.
- Make an **Alchemy S.R. opposed to the original attacker's A.R.** Strictly
  exceed it to neutralize the selected external dose. Failure and tie leave
  the preparation intact; do not reroll or modify the attacker's A.R.
- Alchemy replaces the product's clinical Medicine use for this application;
  no Medicine roll or Medical Kit is required. Ordinary poison handling and
  self-contamination rules remain in force.
- The attack keeps its defense, impact and other normal effects. No physical
  damage is canceled and the weapon is not damaged or disarmed. This does not
  cure internal poison, disable glands/internal secretions, or neutralize a
  later fresh dose.
- A third-party attack is eligible if the same access and reach hold. No
  displacement or projectile interception is granted.
- One attempt per practitioner per attack. Spend one neutralizer dose and
  **3/1 even on failure**. Transformation is Instantaneous, not a maintained
  protection or a promise for the next attack.

## Cost and saved questions

3/1 buys physically executed, narrow reactive utility. It has one chemical
output, requires access and consumable compatibility, and keeps physical
damage live. Comparables: 163 at 3/1 redirects a physical attack; 117 at 2/1
purges one bodily Alteration; 165 at 3/2 reapplies residue after cleanup. Product
cost is real but does not replace Rhythm/Attrition. A stable material outcome
is not an abstract duration surcharge.

Saved minimum cost question, **not an executed experiment**: does 3/1 provide a
useful alternative to ordinary defense/resistance without dominating it when
the neutralizer matches, the point is within 1 meter, the S.R. must beat A.R.,
and even a failed attempt spends a dose? Contrast 2/1 and 4/1. Measure attempted
and successful applications, doses spent, poison delivery prevented, physical
damage received and ATB displacement. Do not count whole attacks canceled or
own damage as guaranteed value.

Derived question families: third-party reach/access; unavailable or incompatible
consumables; tie/failure/success before defense; later reapplication; multiple
external doses with only one selected; internal/innate source exclusion;
projectiles and movement; poison handling risks and quality/severity separation.
