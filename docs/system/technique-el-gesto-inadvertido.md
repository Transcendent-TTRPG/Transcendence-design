## Approved rule

- Bufoni origin, not a racial prerequisite; active utility, Novice or higher Stealth specialization.
- Be Hidden from at least one observer. Declare one brief operation eligible for Interact, meeting ordinary reach, access, equipment and feasibility requirements.
- Have a real physical way to reduce execution signals. The Narrator identifies which signals can be controlled and declares task/environment difficulty before the S.R. This is a self-operation, not a Nature-based imposition upon a creature.
- Roll Stealth and perform the operation in the same execution. The check determines discretion, not whether an otherwise valid ordinary operation occurs.
- On success, only controlled signals of that execution fail to create a provoked detection opportunity, and only for observers from whom the practitioner was already Hidden.
- On failure, the operation still occurs; its signals invoke ordinary detection rules. Failure is not automatic revelation. Where Perception is permitted, it resolves against the existing active Hidden value under the inherited rules.
- Do not reroll, replace or improve that value; do not grant Hidden or erase an observer's previous localization.
- Visible changes to the object, alarms, light, uncontrolled signals, deliberate searches, special senses and loss of physical concealment support still resolve normally.
- Rhythm 3 / Attrition 1 includes the operation, without additionally charging Interact, even on check failure.
- No attack, displacement, extra interaction or replacement of another specialization's task. Instantaneous; the permission ends with this execution, not a later action.

Picking up a loose object is ordinary Interact, not fine theft, disarming or bypassing a held/contested object. Operating a simple latch does not grant lock-picking or Engineering. Quiet handling does not make a noisy alarm inaudible or a visible object invisible. The method does not automatically cancel unrelated reactions; their own trigger, information, targeting and reach requirements still govern them.
