# Backgrounds

Background represents the character's life before adventuring. It determines:
- **Major affinity**: the specialization category where the character progresses faster (5 progress points to level up instead of 10)
- **Starting specializations**: 3 competencies at Level 1 / Rank 1 from restricted categories

> Every character also receives **one Tenacity specialization of choice** as a universal starting specialization (Level 1 / Rank 1), independent of background. Total starting specializations: 4.

## Backgrounds

| Background     | Major Affinity       | Starting Specializations |
|---|---|---|
| Martial Artist | Physical             | 2 Physical + 1 Mental |
| Artisan        | Arts and Crafts      | 2 Arts and Crafts + 1 Lore or 1 Social |
| Wanderer       | Mental               | 2 Mental + 1 Physical |
| Custodian      | Lore                 | 2 Lore + 1 Social or 1 Mental |
| Noble          | Social               | 1 Social + 2 from any other category |

### Martial Artist
Devoted to bodily perfection through combat schools, warrior clans, arenas, or discipline rituals.

### Artisan
Dedicated to transforming matter into useful, beautiful, or dangerous objects.
Smiths, alchemists, tailors, carvers, builders. Values technical, patient, precise observation.

### Wanderer
Formed by the road — no fixed home, or learned to live far from it.
Developed ingenuity, intuition, and adaptation across frontiers, ruins, and foreign cities.

### Custodian
Heir to traditions tasked with preserving knowledge — archives, temples, libraries, guardian orders.
Understands the value of the old; moral weight in preserving or interpreting correctly.

### Noble
Born or raised in an environment of privilege, education, hierarchy, and social ties.
Trained to move through influence structures, negotiation, prestige, and duty.

## Synapsis at Creation

Since all starting specializations begin at Rank 1, they immediately activate Synapsis:
- Each of the 3 background specializations grants +1 to its associated characteristic.
- The universal Tenacity specialization grants +1 to Tenacity.
- Total minimum Synapsis bonus at creation: +4 across characteristics (distribution depends on choices).
